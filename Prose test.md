# Prose Test: Confession to a Girl from Another World

The Night of the Winter Star had descended upon Fontur City with a quiet, crystalline grace. Above the stone spires of the city’s circle, the sky was a deep, velvet indigo, illuminated by the shimmering celestial bodies that gave the festival its name. Small, delicate snowflakes began to drift through the air, coating the iron-wrought benches and the lush gardens in a thin, shimmering layer of white.

Ion Kynne sat on the edge of the bench, her brown hair dusted with frost. Beside her sat Victoria—Vi, as she preferred—the "fancy girl" who had tumbled into Ion’s life like a stray spark from a different reality. Even in the biting winter chill, Vi maintained an effortless, noble elegance, her pale purple hair catching the faint glow of the city’s lights. 

As they stood up to begin the walk back to the warmth of the inn, Ion felt a sudden, thundering pulse in her chest that had nothing to do with her magic. The words she had been holding back for weeks were suddenly pushing against her teeth, demanding to be heard.

"Vi!" Ion’s voice broke the silence, sharper and more desperate than she had intended.

Vi stopped in her tracks, her deep purple eyes turning toward Ion with a look of mild curiosity. "Hmm? What’s up, Ion?"

Ion felt her face heating up, a fierce, "beet-red" blush that the winter air couldn't hope to cool. She clutched the hem of her travel cloak, her knuckles turning white. "I-I know it hasn't been long since you've been here," she began, her voice small and wavering. "And I know it’s way too early to say something like this, but..."

She took a ragged breath, the cold air stinging her lungs.

"W-will you... please go out with me!?"

The silence that followed was deafening. Ion squeezed her eyes shut, waiting for the rejection, for the laughter, or worse—the "friendzone" that had claimed so many others.

"Ah... that..." Vi’s voice was soft, devoid of its usual playful edge.

Ion’s heart sank. *This is it,* she thought, her pulse echoing in her ears like a drum. *Am I being rejected right now??*

"I already knew this was bound to happen," Vi continued, her gaze drifting toward the falling snow. "Yet... it still caught me by surprise. I still haven't quite sorted out my own feelings, so..."

Ion looked up, blinking. Vi wasn't walking away. Instead, the girl who usually carried herself with such untouchable confidence looked... vulnerable. Her cheeks were tinged with a faint, genuine fluster, and for the first time, the "Charming Fox" seemed unsure of her own power.

"It’s not that I don't want to hear those words," Vi whispered, her voice barely audible over the soft rustle of the wind. "I just... I don't know how to feel right now. Can you please... wait for my response?"

A wave of relief and a new, deeper affection washed over Ion. She looked at the girl before her—the noble runaway, the phantom thief, the stranger from another world—and realized she looked more beautiful in this moment of uncertainty than she ever had when she was being "fancy."

"Y-yeah!" Ion blurted out, a wide, loyal smile breaking through her embarrassment. Inside, her resolve hardened into something as "ancient as bricks." *I’ll wait. I’ll definitely wait, no matter how long it takes!*

Vi stared at Ion for a moment, her eyes widening slightly at the sheer sincerity of the response. Slowly, her flustered expression melted into a soft, relieved smile—the kind of smile she usually reserved only for the most private of moments.

"That’s great to hear," Vi said, her voice regaining its melodious, calm tone. "Shall we... head back now?"

Without waiting for an answer, Vi reached out and offered her hand. Ion took it eagerly, her warm palm pressing against Vi’s. As they walked together toward the lights of the inn, their fingers intertwined, the cold of the Winter Star felt a thousand miles away. For Ion, the "wait" had already begun, and it felt like the greatest adventure of her life.
