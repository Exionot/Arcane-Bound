# **Description** 
The **Underworld**, also referred to as **Hell**, is a realm that is exclusively found in the world of [[Arcaena]]. Separate from the overworld or the lands above, it is a place of fire and flames. Devoid of water, streams of molten rock and lava flow into larger lakes no man would dare to go near. It is home to [[Devil|devils]], a species also exclusive to [[Arcaena]].
