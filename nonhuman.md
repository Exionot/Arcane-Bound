Etheric rain poured across the collapsed district. Broken neon flickered beneath sheets of violet static as the remains of the containment walls sparked violently.

The operation had already failed.

VOID operators dragged the wounded toward the extraction carriers while crystalline growths slowly crawled across the streets like veins beneath glass.

> "Containment line E-12 has collapsed!"
> 
> "We're getting spikes from below!"
> 
> "Where's Division 9?!"

Krystalle forced herself upright against a shattered transport.

Her gauntlet flashed red.

`INFECTION LEVEL RISING`

Her vision blurred.

Ahead of her, the underground breach pulsed with black-violet light. Etheric mist spilled upward from the abyss beneath the city.

And standing above it...

Mhax.

Rain rolled down the dark edges of her coat as Void Crystals glowed beneath her skin.

> Mhax: You people never change.
> 
> Mhax: Still abandoning everyone for containment.

Several VOID agents raised their weapons.

Mhax smiled.

The streets trembled.

Suddenly, crystal formations erupted from beneath the road, impaling armored vehicles and tearing operators off their feet.

Screams echoed through the rain.

> "FALL BACK!"
> 
> "WE'RE OVERRUN!"

A command channel burst through the static.

> HQ: All units, authorization granted for emergency quarantine.
> 
> HQ: Seal the district immediately.

Krystalle's eyes widened.

No...

Not again.

Fragments struck her mind.

Rain.

Sirens.

A younger voice shouting into comms.

Containment doors closing.

People screaming behind reinforced glass.

Her breathing staggered.

Then she heard it.

Calm.  
Measured.  
Cold.

> John: Unit 4, reroute power to the northern barricades.
> 
> John: Unit 2, stop firing into the crystals. You're feeding the spread.
> 
> John: Medical carriers move now. Not in thirty seconds. Now.

The battlefield paused.

Operators froze.

Not because of the orders.

Because of the voice delivering them.

John walked through the rain slowly, carrying the same supply case he always brought to operations.

No panic.  
No urgency.

Only precision.

Mhax's expression hardened immediately.

> Mhax: ...
> 
> Mhax: So the old wolf finally came out.

John stopped.

The projector around his body flickered weakly.

Static crawled across his artificial sheep-like ears.

Krystalle stared at him.

Another fragment surfaced violently.

A command room drowning in red alarms.

Him standing beside her.

Not as a resource specialist.

As a commander.

Her lips trembled.

> Krystalle: ...De Luppo...

Silence.

The projector shattered.

The disguise dissolved into fragments of pale light.

The tired old sheep vanished.

Sharp wolf-like ears emerged beneath rain-soaked gray hair. Scars ran across the side of his neck disappearing beneath old tactical armor few people still recognized.

Several older VOID operators visibly recoiled.

One of them whispered under his breath.

> "Ground Zero..."

John looked up toward the etheric storm above the city.

For the first time, the exhaustion disappeared from his posture.

His voice became iron.

> John: This district dies if that breach fully opens.
> 
> John: We have eight minutes.

He grabbed a fallen operator's rifle and checked the chamber in one smooth motion.

Efficient.  
Practiced.  
Ancient.

> John: Krystalle.
> 
> John: Can you still move?

Krystalle stared at him silently.

More memories clawed upward.

Rain-covered containment walls.

His hand on her shoulder.

A younger version of herself shouting:  
_"Commander, there are still civilians inside!"_

John met her gaze.

For a brief second, guilt crossed his face.

Then it vanished beneath battlefield discipline.

> John: You can hate me later.
> 
> John: Right now, we survive.

Mhax laughed softly from across the ruined street.

Not amused.

Furious.

> Mhax: There he is.
> 
> Mhax: The butcher of Rockcore City.

The etheric breach exploded.

John moved first.

Not fast.

Efficient.

Like someone who had already survived the end of the world once before.
