# [[Void Crystal]]
Select one of the effects:
1. The crystals silently eats mana, reducing the magical output of spells.
2. The crystals violently sucks the mana out of a person which could:
	1. Make mana control harder, leading to unintentional high powered spell casts causing mana wasting or spell overcharging.
	2. The user is completely unable to stop a spell cast leading to extreme spell overcharge (in relation to the user's mana) and complete mana depletion.

## Examples
### Effect 1
*Clair casts a stream of flames out of the palm of her hand, small flames jet out her hand and to her surprise, it is much weaker than she expected.*

**Clair:** What the... am I getting weaker?

### Effect 2.1
*Ion and Ted are blocked by a metal electrical door.*

**Ted:** I think you can force these to open by electrocuting it.

*Ion lays her hand on the door's scanner, she releases a small amount of electricity from her hands but to her surprise, a large amount of electricity surges out of her body. The scanner pops, the light bulb burns bright before completely shattering. The power in the building goes out.*

**Ion:** Whoa! That wasn't me!

### Effect 2.2
> [!summary] Ref: ZZZ 1.4, Main Story Chapter 5, Hoshimi Miyabi Tailess Scene  

*Mint points her staff towards the fleeing enemy, she casts a fireball in an attempt to block their exits. However, instead of being able to release the spell, the ball of flame continues to grow larger.*

**Mint:** *What the...*

**Ion:** What are you doing?? 

**Mint:** I don't know what's happening! It won't stop!

*The fireball continues to grow larger and larger, now almost the size of Mint herself.*

**Ion:** Mint! Point UP!

*Mint points her staff straight up into the sky, The fireball still growing to almost twice her size and then suddenly, the spell releases itself, a giant beam of flames shoot out of Mint's staff into the stratosphere. Mint collapses on the spot, her friends rush to her.*
