It was late at night in Aura’s apartment. Aura was laying on the couch, just scrolling through her phone like she usually did to wind down. Everything was quiet until Mint came over and sat right beside her. 

Usually, Mint was the type to be talking or humming, but tonight she was unusually silent. It felt different. Aura noticed it immediately and gave Mint a little nudge with her legs, trying to see what was up.

"Mint? You alright?" Aura asked, looking up from her screen. "You're being unusually silent..."

Mint didn't say anything at first. Instead, she suddenly moved on top of Aura, straddling her and looking right down at her. She had this serious look on her face, but she also looked a little flustered, like she was holding something back.

Aura’s heart started racing. Her face went completely red, and her eyes were jolting everywhere, unable to actually look Mint in the eye. She felt pinned, not just by Mint’s weight, but by the intensity of the moment.

"W-what's wrong?" Aura managed to get out, her voice shaky.

Mint’s serious face suddenly melted into a smile. She didn't explain herself; she just crashed forward, resting her whole weight on top of Aura and wrapping her arms around her in a tight hug.

"I just wanted to rest here," Mint said softly against her.

Aura felt the tension leave her body as she breathed out. She hugged Mint back, letting her hands rest on the back of Mint’s head, gently patting her hair.

"Alright," Aura whispered, finally relaxing into the couch. "You can rest here."
