A breakdown of different mana presence:
### T -1: No Mana
- Synthspireians 
- Mana amount is so little it's safe to say none
- They have absolutely zero mana presence (undetectable for mages)
- Even if they had a positive mana affinity, they wouldn't be able to cast spells without mana from external sources
- If they were to cast a spell alone (without assistance and mana amplifiers) the spell output will be weak and cause an immediate black out due to mana depletion

### T0: Low Presence
- Lowest in Arcaena
- Typical for non-casters
- Casters may be able to use weak spells with limitation before mana depletion
- Detectable by experienced mages

### T1: Normal Presence
- Arcaenian standard
- Typical for human casters
- Detectable by other mages
- Just overall average

### T2: Increased Presence
- Has more mana compared to the previous tier
- Typical for Virken casters
- They may be able to use complex spells at an early age
- Detectable even by beginner mages

### T3: High Presence
- The point where mana ranges from a lot to A LOT
- Typical for non-human casters (e.g. Elves, Dwarves, Devils)
- They are able to cast complex spells at childhood
- Their mana presence is so visible that they often conceal their mana to match normal presence

Mana presence is not set in stone. It can still be increased with proper training

Mana presence does not indicate a person's ability to cast spells. 

A breakdown of the different magic affinities:
### Non-casters
- Rare in Arcaena
- A person who does not believe or able to cast any spells
- Even a person with high mana presence can be a non-caster

### Casters
- A person who can cast spells
- Even Synthspireians can be casters without their knowledge  

Magic affinity is also not set in stone. A change in belief is all it takes to turn a non-caster to a caster and vice versa
