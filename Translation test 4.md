It was the middle of the night, and Vi and Ion were sitting together by a campfire. The flames were flickering as they talked about the day they just had.

"Good job today," Ion said, looking tired.

"Same to you," Vi replied.

"We really got scammed by that haggler," Ion continued. "If you didn't step up, I don't know what I w—"

"Don't move," Vi interrupted.

"H-huh?" Ion stuttered, stopping mid-sentence.

"Just stay still," Vi said.

Vi suddenly slid closer to Ion, leaning her face right in. She had this serious look in her eyes as she got closer and closer. Ion froze, her heart pounding in her chest.

*W-what is she doing?!* Ion thought, her mind racing. *Don't tell me she's about to...*

Suddenly, Vi reached out and snatched something near Ion's head. She pulled back and opened her hand, revealing a small bug.

"There was a critter in your hair," Vi said simply.

"O-oh... there was?" Ion asked, trying to catch her breath.

Vi looked at her more closely. "You alright? Your face is red."

"Y-yeah... I was just startled," Ion said, her face getting even hotter.

Vi gave her a knowing look. "Don't tell me... were you expecting something else?"

"!! N-no! Definitely not!" Ion shouted, jumping up. She was completely flustered now. "Gahh! I'm going to sleep now! A-and you shouldn't stay up so late too!"

Ion quickly turned around and rushed toward her tent without looking back. Once she was inside, she collapsed onto her bedroll, her face still burning.

*I just don't know what to do every time she's that close to me,* Ion thought, her heart still hammering against her ribs. *Aahhh! My heart won't calm down!*
