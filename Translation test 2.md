The apartment was quiet, the kind of heavy, artificial silence that only exists in the late-night heart of Fluxpoint City. Outside, the neon glow of the district hummed, but inside, the only light came from the cool, blue-white radiance of Aura’s phone. She was stretched out on the couch, her thumb rhythmically scrolling through data as she tried to decompress from a day of logic and mechanical precision.

The shift in the room was subtle at first. A shadow detached itself from the hallway and moved toward her. Mint sat down on the edge of the cushions, right beside Aura’s hip. 

Usually, Mint was a whirlwind of soft conversation or playful humming, but tonight, she was a statue. Aura felt the weight of that silence—it was heavy, deliberate, and entirely uncharacteristic. She lowered her phone just an inch, her gaze flicking toward the girl beside her. After a moment, she reached out with her leg, giving Mint a gentle, inquiring nudge.

"Mint?" Aura’s voice was low, careful not to shatter the fragile peace of the room. "You alright? You're being... unusually silent."

She didn't get a verbal answer. Instead, the world suddenly shifted.

In one fluid, athletic motion, Mint moved. She climbed onto the couch, her weight pressing into Aura as she straddled her, looking down with a face that was uncharacteristically grave. Her expression was a complex map of intensity—serious, focused, and yet there was a telltale heat, a flustered pink dusting her cheeks that betrayed her composure.

Aura felt her heart hammer against her ribs, a sudden, violent staccato. Her "stone-cold" commander persona, the one that could face down corrupt officials and military threats without blinking, dissolved in an instant. Her face went hot, a deep, undeniable red blooming across her skin. Her eyes darted everywhere—to the ceiling, to the far wall, to the shadows in the corner—anywhere but the steady, burning gaze of the girl pinning her down.

"W-what's wrong?" Aura managed to stammer, her voice cracking under the sudden, electric pressure of Mint’s proximity.

For a heartbeat, the air between them felt thin, charged with everything they hadn't said. Then, as quickly as the tension had coiled, it snapped into something else. Mint’s serious mask broke into a soft, radiant smile. She didn't say a word as she collapsed forward, her entire weight "crashing" onto Aura’s chest. She wrapped her arms around the taller girl, burying her face into the crook of Aura’s neck in a desperate, clinging hug.

"I just wanted to rest here," Mint whispered against her skin.

Aura’s breath, which she hadn't realized she was holding, left her in a long, shaky exhale. The panic receded, replaced by a warmth that was far more dangerous because of how comfortable it felt. She slowly brought her arms up, mirroring the embrace, and began to rhythmically pat the back of Mint’s head, her fingers brushing against the soft, mint-green hair.

"Alright," Aura said, her voice finally steadying as she accepted the weight. "You can rest here."

Outside, the city continued its cold, technological grind, but on the couch, the commander and the healer had found the only sanctuary that mattered. Mint had claimed her territory, and Aura had finally stopped trying to look away.
