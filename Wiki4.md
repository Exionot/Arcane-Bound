## History

### Background

**Mint Evergreen** is a [[Virken]] mage from [[Lenben Village]]. She is a former member of the [[Guild of Arcane Healers]] and served as the village's healer before pursuing magic. She traveled with the [[Wandering Traders of Arcaena]] in the early days of her journey to learn the arts of magic. [[Ion Kynne]] later recruited her to join the Wanderers, but she refused in order to continue on her own path. Mint went on solo expeditions through the kingdoms of [[Arcaena]], taking quests in exchange for magical artifacts and grimoires — including a commission from former king [[Damascus Verdthorne]].

### Early Childhood

Mint was disallowed by her parents from learning offensive magic, a tradition in their village rooted in caution. They enrolled her in the [[Academy of Healers]] to learn [[Elemental Magic#Vitalis|Vitalis]], where she later became a member of the [[Guild of Arcane Healers]]. Though not in favor of this decision, she complied with the village tradition.

Despite being taught healing magic, Mint treated her training as though she were learning offensive magic. This gave her a clear advantage — her mana control surpassed most young mages, and she could cast healing spells faster than nearly all beginners.

### Young Adolescence

After more than half a decade in the Guild of Arcane Healers, Mint craved to learn real magic and reach her true potential. Her parents disagreed and withdrew their support. Despite this, Mint pursued the art of magic, determined to become as powerful as one of [[Arcaena]]'s [[Ace Mages]].

She was recruited multiple times by [[Ion Kynne]] to join the [[Wandering Traders of Arcaena|Wanderers]] and even accompanied one of their trips across Arcaena. Ultimately she refused full membership, preferring to explore freely, learn new spells, and take quests in exchange for tomes and grimoires.

### During The Convergence

Mint was transported to the technologically advanced world of [[Fluxpoint City]], where she found and met [[Aura Brillfer]].^[[[The Convergence]]] She was taken in by Aura to hide from [[Protection Of Local Livelihood|P.O.L.L.]] agents who were ordered to apprehend Arcaenians.^[[[Her house]]]

## Appearance

### General

A human with feline-like ears and tail. She has long mint-green hair with emerald-like eyes. She wears a total of three piercings across both ears — two on the left, one on the right.

### Outfits

**In [[Arcaena]]**
Her hairstyle is a big singular braid. She wears a short light blue poncho with gold accents and a hood over a black long-sleeved shirt. A gold leaf clip adorns her hair, and a gold cat enamel pin holds her poncho together.

**In [[Synthspire]]**
She wears a black shirt and a light blue jacket with matching black shorts, given to her by [[Aura Brillfer|Aura]]. Her hair was dyed a pale-black color to hide her identity in Synthspire.

## Personality

Mint is seen as kind and helpful in [[Lenben Village|Lenben]] — well disciplined and determined in pursuing her goals. On a serious day she gets tasks done quickly and is efficient at multitasking, making use of her tail as an extra hand.

The majority of the time she is a playful and wholesome feline. She plays with the village children and teaches them magic, though she can be carefree and sometimes acts childish. She is also deeply curious about her surroundings, always eager to learn and discover magic never seen before.

## Factions

### Guild of Arcane Healers

> **Full Note:** [[Guild of Arcane Healers]]

Mint was part of the [[Guild of Arcane Healers]] during her early childhood. She was one of the best healers in the guild, casting healing magic like second nature — helping people in need and teaching young mages healing spells for several years. After years of service, she finally decided she wanted to learn magic beyond what she had been taught. She graduated as one of the best healers the guild had ever trained.

### Wandering Traders of Arcaena

Despite being recruited multiple times by [[Ion Kynne]] and joining one of their trips, Mint ultimately declined full membership in favor of independent exploration.
