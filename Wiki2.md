**Mint Evergreen** is a [[Virken]] (Feline) mage hailing from [[Lenben Village]] in the [[Verdthorne Kingdom]] of [[Arcaena]]. She is a former Master Healer of the [[Guild of Arcane Healers]] and honorary member of the [[Wandering Traders of Arcaena]], who left her post as the village healer to pursue the full breadth of arcane magic. After years of solo expeditions across [[Arcaena]], she was caught in [[The Convergence]] and found herself in the technologically advanced city of [[Fluxpoint City]], where she came to know [[Aura Brillfer]].

> [!character]
> **"Your bed is so fluffy! I want to stay in it forever!"**
> — Mint Evergreen, on Aura's bed

## Background

Mint grew up in [[Lenben Village]] under parents who, following village tradition, forbade her from learning offensive magic. They enrolled her in the [[Academy of Healers]] where she studied [[Elemental Magic#Vitalis|Vitalis]] and eventually rose to the rank of Master Healer within the [[Guild of Arcane Healers]]. Though she complied, she treated her healing training with the same rigor one would apply to combat magic — resulting in mana control and casting speed that far outpaced her peers.

After more than half a decade with the Guild, Mint resolved to pursue real magic and reach her full potential. Against her parents' wishes and without their support, she left [[Lenben Village]] to seek out the arcane arts at their furthest reaches. Over the course of her travels, she was repeatedly recruited by [[Ion Kynne]] to join the [[Wandering Traders of Arcaena]] — she even accompanied one of their cross-[[Arcaena]] expeditions — but refused each time, preferring to roam freely. She funded her studies by taking quests in exchange for tomes, grimoires, and magical artifacts, eventually earning a commission from the former king of [[Verdthorne Kingdom]], [[Damascus Verdthorne|King Damascus Verdthorne]].

### During The Convergence

> *Main article: [[The Convergence]]*

Mint was transported to [[Fluxpoint City]], a technologically advanced world far removed from [[Arcaena]].

> *See narrative: [[Mint Evergreen Narratives Manuscript]]*

There she encountered [[Aura Brillfer]], who took her in and sheltered her from [[Protection Of Local Livelihood|P.O.L.L.]] agents tasked with apprehending displaced Arcaenians.

## Appearance

Mint has a human-like build with feline ears and a tail, characteristic of Feline-type [[Virken]]. She has long mint-green hair, emerald-coloured eyes, and three piercings across both ears — two on the left, one on the right.

| **In Synthspire** | Black shirt, light blue jacket, and matching black shorts — provided by [[Aura Brillfer\|Aura]]. Hair dyed pale-black to conceal her identity. |

## Personality

In [[Lenben Village]], Mint is known as kind, disciplined, and dependable — someone who gets tasks done with focus and efficiency, even making use of her tail for multitasking. Away from duty, she reveals a more playful and wholesome side: she plays with village children, teaches them simple magic, and can occasionally slip into a carefree or even childish mood. She is deeply driven by curiosity — always chasing magic she has never seen before and places she has yet to explore.

> [!quote]
> *"She is a playful and wholesome feline. Though, she can be carefree from time to time."*

## Affiliations

### Guild of Arcane Healers

> *Main article: [[Guild of Arcane Healers]]*

Mint joined the [[Guild of Arcane Healers]] during early childhood and remained one of their most skilled healers for years — casting healing magic with a speed and precision that earned her the rank of **Master Healer**. She spent her time in the guild healing people in need and mentoring young mages. When she ultimately decided to pursue broader magic, she graduated from the guild on good terms as one of their finest alumni.

### Wandering Traders of Arcaena

> *Main article: [[Wandering Traders of Arcaena]]*

Despite multiple recruitment attempts by [[Ion Kynne]] and even joining one of their journeys across [[Arcaena]], Mint declined full membership in the [[Wandering Traders of Arcaena|Wanderers]], preferring the freedom of solo travel. She holds **Honorary Member** status.

---

## Relations

### Aura Brillfer

> *Main article: [[Aura Brillfer]]*

The only person who has come to mean more to Mint than magic itself. She is Mint's lover — the one who sheltered her during [[The Convergence]] in an unfamiliar world.

### Ion Kynne

> *Main article: [[Ion Kynne]]*

A recurring figure in Mint's journey. [[Ion Kynne]] has extended membership offers to Mint multiple times, and the two have crossed paths throughout her travels across [[Arcaena]].
