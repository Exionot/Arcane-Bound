When someone is infected with [[Null's Disease]], their body naturally tries to break down the crystals forming in their body, and when these crystals break they release energy then dissipate into nothing. 

During the early stages of the disease this is not a worry since the body can still accommodate the occasional tiny gaps and heals faster than the crystals death. However, on later stages when an individual has a high percentage of crystals in their body, it is usually in large clusters, and when those clusters breaks down, it will lead to holes internally and or externally which can lead to deadly outcomes such as organ failure and internal bleeding. This can be worsened by a person's void density.

On some cases where a person dies during the late stage of the disease with a high void density, examination of their bodies show them with holes where the crystals grew, almost looking like they've been hollowed from the inside.

## Void-to-body Percentage (V%)
- 1-10V% - Early 
- 11-30V% - Middle
- 31-50V% - Late

## Void Density (u/L)
- 0.1-0.15u/L - Slow spread
- 0.16-0.49u/L - Normal spread
- 0.5-0.8u/L - Fast spread
- 0.81u/L above - Certain death

Void density increases as void percentage worsen.
