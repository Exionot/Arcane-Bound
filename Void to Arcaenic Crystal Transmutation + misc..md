Void is called Crystalline Ether
Arcaenic is called "Crystal from the Ether"

Void takes mana
Arcaenic amplifies mana(but also radiates it)

Small cluster of void quietly takes very small amount of mana
Large cluster of void forcibly takes mana

When void is broken it releases ether energy(mana it consumed)
When Arcaenic is broken it also releases ether energy(but more)

When void breaks down in an infected person the energy(mana) stays with the person but the amount of mana is so small that it cant slow down the infection(also void density is a factor)

Arcaenic is the stable version of void

When void intakes `x` amount of mana it turns into Arcaenic

Mage can flow raw mana(no spell cast) to void via Astraxia to perform Arcaenic transmutation(large clusters can trigger death siphon) 
Large clusters are dealt with by flowing raw mana to void via Arcana(smaller risk of death siphon)
