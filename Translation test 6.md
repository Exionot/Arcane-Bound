Sol Capital was bustling with activity as Vi and Ion strolled through the streets together. The sun was setting, casting long shadows across the pavement. Ion was in a playful mood, leaning toward the girl beside her as they walked.

**Ion:** Let me ask you this, fancy girl.
**Vi:** It's "Vi".
**Ion:** Have you ever had a boyfriend?
**Vi:** No.
**Ion:** Girlfriend?
**Vi:** Nope.
**Ion:** Any romantic interest whatsoever?
**Vi:** Not to my knowledge, no.
**Vi:** But some of my friends do call me charismatic.
**Ion:** You? Hahaha! You're so tiny, how can anyone see you and fall for you?

Ion's laugh was cut short. Before she could even process the words, Vi’s hand shot out and grabbed Ion’s arm. With a sudden burst of the speed she was known for, Vi pulled her into a quiet, narrow alleyway. Before Ion could protest, Vi pinned her against the stone wall, leaning in until her face was just inches away.

**Vi:** Like this.

Vi didn't pull back. She stayed right there, her hand coming up to rest on Ion’s face. The cool stone of the wall was at Ion’s back, but the air between them was suddenly, uncomfortably warm. Vi leaned in further, whispering directly into Ion’s ear. Ion’s face started to flush, the heat rising rapidly.

**Vi:** Are you... feeling flustered yet?

Ion felt like she was suffocating under the intensity of Vi’s gaze. She suddenly pushed Vi away, taking a few quick steps back and panting for air like she’d just run a mile.

**Ion:** N-nope! I-it's not effective at all!
**Vi:** Are you sure? Your face is beet red.
**Ion:** S-shut up! You just caught me off guard, that's all!

Ion quickly turned away, trying to hide her face as they stepped back into the busy street. But the heat in her cheeks told a very different story from the one she was telling Vi.
