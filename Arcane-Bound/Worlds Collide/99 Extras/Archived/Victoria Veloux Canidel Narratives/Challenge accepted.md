**Character/s**:
- [[Victoria Veloux Canidel]]
- [[Ion Kynne]]

**Location**: Outside of [[Rhilekos City]]
**Time of Occurrence**: 2405, Early Winter

*Vi and the Wandering Traders are outside of Rhilekos City where Vi and Ion are about to have a duel.*

**Ion:** Let's see how long you can hold up against magic. 

*Ion cast an electric projectile towards Vi. Vi narrowly dodges and tries to close the distance. Vi throws a punch but it goes through Ion.*

**Vi:** What the...

*Vi gets zapped by electricity, she turns her head and sees Ion with a playful look on her face.*

**Ion:** I'm not there little fox.

*Before Vi could move, another voice from behind calls her.*

**Ion:** Over here!
**Vi:** *She's multiplying? This has to an illusion of some kind.*

*Multiple Ion's appear around her, confused, Vi looks around to determine which is the real Ion.*

**Ion:** Scared? Don't tell me you're backing out now?

*The Ion's begin circling around her, slowly getting closer. In that moment, Vi's ears shifts, sensing something behind her.*

**Vi:** *I got you now!*

*Vi turns around and throws a punch at one of the Ion's, landing a hit, pushing Ion back. The other Ion's disappear as she gets back up.*

**Ion:** Impressive, you have some sharp senses.
**Ion:** However, this fight isn't over until one of us concedes.

*Ion unleashes a bombardment of electricity heading straight towards Vi. Vi activates her weapon and slashes the bolt of electricity approaching her, it deflects and hits the floor instead.*

**Vi:** Hm.. I guess that works.

*Vi begins to run forward slashing and dodging incoming attacks, she goes behind Ion, snatching her staff in the process disarming her, and pins her to the ground holding both of her arms.*

**Ion:** This would've worked but, you're fighting a mage.

*Ion cast electricity out of her hands electrocuting Vi. She reverses the situation, pinning Vi to the ground.*

**Ion:** Ready to concede?
**Vi:** No, but you will be.

*Vi pulls Ion away with her legs, kicking her back. Vi shoots out wires from a hidden weapon strapped to her arms. The wires wrap around Ion effectively immobilizing her.*

**Ion:** You have a lot of tricks up your sleeves, little fox. Alright, I concede, you win. 
**Ion:** Now, can you get these off of me.


> [!NOTE] Notes
> Some things to point out:
> - Ion used immediately used illusions because she knew Vi cannot sense mana would've gave it away.
> - Vi manage to determine which Ion was real was because illusions, though look indistinguishable from the original, does not interact with the real world the same hence, no footsteps. Vi noticed this and traced the sound of the footstep to locate the real Ion.
