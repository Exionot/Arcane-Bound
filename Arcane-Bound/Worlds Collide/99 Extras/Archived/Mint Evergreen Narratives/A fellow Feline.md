*It is the afternoon. Mint finds herself in what it seems to be the center of a bustling city; the area is loud, people walk by at every moment. Their outfits are more diverse; almost casual yet different from Arcaenian fashion.*

**Mint:** I really need to find a way to get out of here...

*Mint remains lost. She looks through the crowd and sees someone. Ears and tail just like hers, a fellow feline is within her sight. Mint squeezes against the crowd catching up to the feline she saw. She calls out to her.*

**Mint:** E-excuse me!

*The feline is walking with bodyguards. Hearing Mint's call, she stops walking and turns around. Before Mint could speak, the feline's bodyguards raise and point their weapons towards Mint.*

**Bodyguard:** Back off, civilian!

*Mint steps back, and before she could summon her weapon, the feline commands her bodyguards to lower their weapons.*

*Mint focuses and checks the feline for any traces of mana. But to no avail, there's nothing, not even her bodyguards have any traces of mana.*

**Aura:** Are you lost?
**Mint:** Ah! Yes, it seems like I got suddenly transported here. 
**Mint:** Where are we right now?
**Aura:** This is Amphere Square of Fluxpoint City.
**Mint:** I didn't know Arcaena had such developed cities like this.

*The feline makes a gesture to her bodyguards, they salute her and begin to walk away, leaving Mint alone with her.*

**Aura:** Follow me.

*Mint, desperate to go home, nods and follows her.*

**Mint:** *She might have answers to what's happening so I can get home.*
