**Mint:** *She's been visiting quite often. I guess they really were close friends in the past.*

*Renze takes out another box of pastries and opens them, it is filled with strawberry sprinkled donuts.*

**Renze:** Tada! I brought a box of your favorites too!

*Renze grabs one donut from the box and holds it near Aura's mouth.*

**Renze:** Open wide! 
**Mint:** *She's feeding her??*
**Aura:** Seriously... I'm gonna gain weight from all of this.
**Renze:** Oh come on, every now and then is fine!
**Renze:** Right Mint?
**Mint:** Ah, right. You always work hard so it shouldn't be a problem!
**Aura:** Alright, fine...

*Aura opens her mouth and takes a bite of the donut Renze was holding. For a second, Mint tenses up before standing,*

**Mint:** I-I'm going to take a stroll.
**Aura:** Are you sure? It's going to be dark soon.
**Mint:** I'll be back before dinner.
**Aura:** Alright, take care then.

*Mint leaves the apartment and walks around Amphere Square alone.*

**Mint:** *What am I doing? It's way too cold to be taking strolls right now.*
**Mint:** *But if I stayed there I would just end up being...*
**Mint:** *Aghh! Why am I getting mad at her?! She's her friend, and I'm just an outsider that doesn't even belong in this world!*
**Mint:** *Sigh* *I wonder if Ion and the others are looking for me...*

*Mint returns to the apartment and finds Aura waiting for her in the sofa.*

**Aura:** Welcome back, done with your walk?
**Mint:** Yeah...
**Mint:** Where's Renze?
**Aura:** She left shortly after you went out.
**Aura:** Sit down here.

*Mint sits down next to Aura in the sofa. Silence fills the room, until, Aura places her hand on the top of Mint's head in-between her ears and starts petting her.*

**Aura:** Is something bothering you?

*Mint flinches in surprise, too shocked to speak. Aura immediately takes her hand off of Mint.*

**Aura:** Oh, do you not like that? I'm sorry.
**Mint:** No, I was just taken by surprise... I like it when someone pats my head...

*Aura places her hand back into Mint's head and starts to pat her again.*

**Aura:** Just tell me when somethings bothering you, okay?
**Mint:** Okay.
**Mint:** *I was angry earlier, but now, she makes me feel at ease, as if what I felt earlier never occurred...*
