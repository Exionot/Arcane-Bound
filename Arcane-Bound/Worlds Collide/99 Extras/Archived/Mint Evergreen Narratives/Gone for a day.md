*Mint arrives at Lenben Village. as she enters the village she bumps into Clay, a friend of hers.*

**Clay:** Hey Mint! Back from your adventures already?
**Mint:** Oh, I didn't go adventuring.
**Clay:** You were gone all day yesterday, little Rosy thought you went away again without telling her.

*Over in the distance a shout of a small voice can be heard calling out Mint's name.*

**Rosy:** Is that big sis Mint???

*A small Canis Virken pup runs towards Mint with excitement. Rosy hugs Mint tightly. Mint pets her head as she talked to Clay.*

**Mint:** Did anything weird happen when I was away?
**Clay:** Nothing really, Kesh"s dock collapsed yesterday but that's pretty much it. I told him many times already, his dock was rotting.
**Mint:** *Looks like none of the other villagers were transported. I should probably catch up to the wanderers before they leave.* 
**Rosy:** Play with me Mint!

*Mint squats down to be on eye level with Rosy*

**Mint:** Sorry Rosy, but big sis is going to meet up with Ion. Remember her?
**Rosy:** The very beautiful girl?? Can I go too??
**Mint:** But it's so far! Your papa probably won't let me take to far places yet.
**Clay:** Oh, you're leaving again?
**Mint:** Yeah, I have some catching up to do with the wanderers.
**Mint:** Don't worry little Rosy, we'll play a lot when I get back okay?
**Rosy:** Okay!!

*Mint stands back up as she prepares to leave.*

**Mint:** Well, I'll be going now.
**Clay:** Good luck with your travels!
**Rosy:** Goodbye Mint!!

*Mint waves her goodbyes to Clay and Rosy as she exits the village heading towards Rhilekos City*
