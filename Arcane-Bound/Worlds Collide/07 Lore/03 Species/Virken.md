## Etymology
**Virken** comes from the root word **Vircus** where *Vir-* comes from the Latin word *Vir* meaning "Man" and *-cus* comes from the Latin word *Pecus* meaning "Beast" or "Animal". Continuous usage of this word caused it to change into *Virc* and slowly turned it into *Virken*.
