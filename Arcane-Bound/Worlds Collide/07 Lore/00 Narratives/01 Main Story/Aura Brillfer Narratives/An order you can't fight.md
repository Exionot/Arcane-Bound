**Character/s**: [[Aura Brillfer]], [[Mint Evergreen]]
**Location**: [[Amphere Square]]
**Time of Occurrence**: 2405, Early Winter
**Related:** [[A fellow Feline]]

*Its the afternoon, the sun is at it's peak. Aura is doing her last patrol around Circuit District at Amphere Square with her bodyguards. Her in-ear radio sparks to life.*

**Radio:** Point Alpha, identify yourself.

**Aura:** This is Commander Brillfer speaking.

**Radio:** Are you still in patrol?

**Aura:** We're just about to wrap up here in Amp Square, why?

**Radio:** Be advised, we currently have a code two. Apprehend anyone you see wearing a "fictional" looking outfit.

**Aura:** Do we know why we're arresting them?

**Radio:** They're believed to be part of an unknown gang and could be connected to the recent missing incidents.

**Aura:** Believed? We do not have a strong evidence and we're arresting them?

**Radio:** Just do as I say Commander Brillfer, this is an order you can't fight. Intel came from the higher ups.

**Aura:** Alright, alright, I'll see what I can do.

*Radio closes the connection, Aura lets out a big sigh.*

**Bodyguard:** What did they say commander?

**Aura:** It's nothing important, just keep an eye out for suspicious individuals.

*As they were about to continue in their patrol, a voice come from behind.*

**Mint:** E-excuse me!
