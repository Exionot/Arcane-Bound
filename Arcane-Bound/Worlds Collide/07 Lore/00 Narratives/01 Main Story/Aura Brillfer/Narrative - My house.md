*Aura and Mint are walking the streets of Circuit District, Aura glances at Mint, she notices unfamiliarity and fascination in her eyes.*

*They go inside a building complex to Aura's apartment.*

**Mint:** Uhm... Where are we going exactly?
**Aura:** This is where I live.
**Mint:** Oh, I thought you were just going to tell me how to get out of the city.
**Aura:** The city is way to big to get to any exit gates by foot, and it gets really cold during winter nights, don't you want a place to pass the night? Your clothes look roughed up too.
**Aura:** *Plus I can't let the other agents see you like that.*

*Aura opens the door to her apartment.*

**Mint:** Whoa... this is nothing like the inns I've been to. 
**Aura:** *Inns? She's talking nonsense again.*

*Mint lies on Aura's couch and is amazed by it's softness.*

**Aura:** It's just a couch, have you not tried one before?
**Mint:** This is the softest couch I've ever been on! Not even the King's couch can beat this one!
**Aura:** *King?? There had been no monarchies since the first civilization... I can't tell if she's playing an act or something else.*
**Aura:** *Fine... Time for me get information from you.*

*Aura stands by the door watching as Mint fascinates over the mode simple of objects in her apartment.*
