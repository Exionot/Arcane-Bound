**Rem:** Welcome to Starveil Retrieval Services! What can we retrieve for you?

*Anxious, Clair approaches the front desk.*

**Clair:** Um... I would like to look for this boo- Ack!

*Whispers forcibly fills Clair's mind. Unbearable pain surges through her head, both hands on her ears as she drops to the floor.*

**Rem:** Miss! Are you alright?!

*Rem rushes to Clair, bringing her to the lobby chairs.*

*Clair raises her head, her eyes lit up with an eerie glow.*

**Clair:** Interesting... How about we have some fun in this new world?~

**Clair:** Oh really? Almost the peak of dusk, not a single person in sight besides you and me, would anyone even notice if you disappeared?
