*Ion and Ted are at Yushinami Stakes Course to watch a virken debut race. The course is lightly crowded, they stand near the front to get a clear view. The race approaches it's beginning.*

**Commentator:** It is the dawn of a new year, new contenders have stepped foot are here in Yushinami Stakes Course. 1600 meters of turf lies before them, will they be able to begin their career with a success in the Starter Stakes today?!

*The racers walk up to the starting gate, their path blocked by the ground protruding from the floor.*

**Commentator:** All 8 racers are in position. We wait for the start.

*Everyone is focused in the starting gate waiting for the race to begin. Suddenly, the ground drops down, opening the racers' path.*

**Commentator:** And they're off! Everyone seems to have a good start.
**Commentator:** Will today's favorite, Still Thunder live up to everyone's expectation?
**Ion:** How is this even fun? We're just watching virkens run.
**Ted:** If it's the talk of town everywhere, there's has to be something that pulls people to watch.

*As Ion and Ted wonder about the charm of the sport, the racers runs towards the third corner.*

**Commentator:** Still Thunder is currently in the middle of the pack. 500 meters left to go, will she be able to break through?!
**Commentator:** We reach the final corner! Unknown Grace is leading the pack effortlessly! In second we have Charming Daisy.
**Commentator:** Will the favorite, Still Thunder, make a break for it?!
**Ion:** Come on, the winner is clearly that runner, her lead is way too big.

*In a blink of an eye, Still Thunder comes from the in from the outside of the pack slowly passing the leading racers.*

**Ted:** Oh, look at that girl, she's fast.
**Commentator:** Here she comes! Still Thunder breaks through from the outside! 200 meters left! Will she be able to take the victory?!

*Ion's eyes open wide in surprise. The crowd's cheer loudens as the races reach the finish line. She slowly leans forward in anticipation.*

**Commentator:** Still Thunder and Unknown Grace are head to head! 
**Commentator:** Can she make it?! Can she make it?! Can she make it?! Can she make it?!

*The ground is shaken by the runner's strides, the crowd's cheer slowly fades into background silence, time seems to slow down. Ion watched as Still Thunder takes the lead by a small margin.*

**Commentator:** Still Thunder!! She takes the win by a neck! 
**Commentator:** Still Thunder wins the Starter Stakes!
**Ion:** So cool...

*The crowd's cheer brings Ion back to her senses.*

**Ted:** I can't believe she managed to pull that off!
**Ion:** She's so cool!! How did she do it?? I want to run too!! Hey Ted, how about we become runners instead?!
**Ted:** Looks like someone is a fan...

*Ted slowly nudges Ion towards the exit.*

**Ted:** Come on now, that sport is exclusive to equus virkens. Let's go, we still need to switch cities.
**Ion:** Aww...
**Ion:** We need to watch her next race!!
**Ted:** If we manage to finish wandering in this region we'll come back here.
