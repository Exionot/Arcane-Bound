**Character/s**:
- [[Victoria Veloux Canidel]]
- [[Ion Kynne]]
- [[Ted Ursa]]
- [[Still Thunder]]

**Location**: [[Rhilekos City]]
**Time of Occurrence**: 2405, Early Winter

*Vi and Ted are walking on the path towards Rhilekos City. The sun is at it's peak, illuminating the path with it's rays.*

**Vi:** Do you really think the guild will let me join?
**Ted:** Of course they would, they're not really picky of who we drag along as long you that you're capable.
**Vi:** You've been missing from the guild right? Will they believe the transportation thing?
**Ted:** Well... it's a world of magic after all, the concept of being transported to another world shouldn't be as crazy as it sounds to them.

*Vi and Ted arrive at Rhilekos City. The city is busy with merchants, some sell potions, artifacts, and even weapons, carriage horses walk through the stone roads as Ted guide Vi through the wide streets of Rhilekos. They arrive closer to the guild's location as Ted spots the guild's carriage. Ted shouts from afar to grab the guild's attention, a girl notices and starts running towards Ted. With her arms wide open, the girl runs at full speed towards him, the moment she reaches Ted, she hugs him like a child seeing her parents after ages.*

**Ion:** TED!! We've been looking for you everywhere!
**Ted:** Okay, okay, settle down, let's talk about this in the carriage.

*The girl lets go of Ted and sees Vi standing beside him.*

**Ion:** Oh? Who's this girl?
**Ion:** Gah! Don't tell me you got tired of us and joined another guild??
**Ted:** No, no, of course not! Come on, to the carriage we go.

*Ted gently nudges the girl to walk back to the carriage, as they walk back, Ted looks at Vi telling her to follow. Ted tries to explains to the guild what had happened when he disappeared.*

**Ion:** YOU GOT TRANSPORTEED TO A DIFFERENT WORLD??
**Ion:** Are you sure you didn't accidentally stepped into a portal?
**Ted:** I don't think Arcaena has a city with skyscrapers in every corner...
**Ion:** So who's this girl you brought then? You didn't kidnap her from the other world did you?
**Ted:** No I didn't! Why does everyone think I kidnapped her? 
**Ted:** This is Vi. She found me when I got brought back to Brickstone Town. She's not a native here, she also got transported from the other world to here. Vi, this is Ion, she kind of the one in charge here.
**Ion:** Is that why she doesn't have a smidge of mana in her?
**Vi:** *Mana? Is that where their powers come from?*
**Ted:** Is that so? I never noticed. Vi, is that why your people don't have magical abilities?
**Vi:** I guess so?
**Ted:** Well, with everything on the side, I'm actually planning to add her to the guild. She's a fast one! She out sped me when I challenged her to a race.
**Still Thunder:** So... like average speed?
**Ted:** Hey! I've been picking up speed recently too!
**Ted:** Oh yeah, Vi, this is Still Thunder, she's also super fast just like you!

*Ion judgingly looks at Vi.*

**Ion:** Can she even defend herself?
**Ted:** Of course she can.
**Ion:** She doesn't have any weapons or magic capabilities, how can she defend herself?
**Ted:** Oh you're not ready for this, show her your weapon, Vi.

*Vi raises her hand, she closes her fist and from the gold housing protruding from her gloves, blades made of solid light come out. It faintly glows with a gentle hum.*

**Ted:** They call it "technology" in their world.
**Ion:** Interesting... let's see how well your "technology" holds up against magic.
**Ted:** Come on Ion, she's clearly at a disadvantage.
**Ion:** Well, she needs to fight it out if wants to be in the guild doesn't she? 

*Ion summons her staff and points it at Vi.*

**Ion:** I challenge you to a duel! Win and you get to be part of our guild!
