**Character/s**: [[Mint Evergreen]], [[Ted Ursa]], [[Victoria Veloux Canidel]]
**Location**: [[Vilebloom Forest]], Outside of [[Lenben Village]]
**Time of Occurrence**: 2405, Early Winter
**Related**: [[Feline encounter]]

*Mint is lost in the woods of Vilebloom Forest. She continues to walk, looking for a path she can follow, and then she saw it.*

**Mint:** *Finally a path! I can finally go home now.*

*Mint enters the dirt path out from the bushes. As she brushes herself off, Mint glances to her left, a bear is approaching her at full sprint.*

**Mint:** H-hey wait! Slow down!

*In a blink of an eye, mint summons her staff and casts a barrier in front of her. Not being able to stop in time, the bear hits the barrier, the impact pushes Mint a step back.*

**Mint:** What... are you doing?! Why are you running so fast in these narrow paths-

*Mint takes a closer look at the bear and recognizes him.*

**Mint:** Ted?! What are you doing here??

**Mint:** *Maybe he wasn't transported after all?*

**Ted:** Mint! I'm so sorry! What are you doing here? We're actually rushing to your house.

**Mint:** We?

*Mint looks to the side of Ted and sees a Vulpes Virken standing behind him. The girl stood elegantly. Mint approaches her.*

**Mint:** Whoa... You're a fancy girl, I can feel it. Which royal family are you fro-

**Mint:** Ted... Ion came to me looking for you a couple days ago. You didn't kidnap a member of the royalties and disguised it as "going back for phthalo roots" did you?

**Ted:** No, of course not! This is Vi, she actually found me back in Brickstone, she's not of any royalties. Well... at least not in this world.

**Mint:** *Not in this world? She's not from here? Was she transported from the other world to here?*

**Mint:** Did both of you also got transported suddenly?

**Ted:** Wait, how did you know we got transported?

**Mint:** I got transported to a different world as well.

**Ted:** You got transported too?? How come i did not saw you in the detention area?

**Mint:** *He got locked up? I guess Aura wasn't lying after all.*

**Mint:** A nice lady took me in. 

**Mint:** *Well after she tried to fight me that is...*

**Mint:** She said people were after us.

**Ted:** Wait, why us? We haven't done anything.

**Mint:** *That's what I'm wondering too...*

**Mint:** You should probably head straight to Rhilekos City for now, You've been missing for more than a week now, Ion said they would stay there until they find you.

**Ted:** Are you not going with us?

**Mint:** I need to check on the village first, just to make sure nobody there got transported too.

**Mint:** I'll try to catch up with you after I check the village.

*Mint and Ted wave they goodbyes as they split up their paths.*
