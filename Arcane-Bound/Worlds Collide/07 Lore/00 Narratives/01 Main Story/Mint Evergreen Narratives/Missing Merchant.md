**Character/s**: [[Mint Evergreen]], [[Ion Kynne]]
**Location**: [[Lenben Village]]
**Time of Occurrence**: 2405, 28th of Late Autumn

*It is a peaceful morning in Lenben. Mint is tending to her plants when suddenly, a familiar voice shouts her name. Mint turns around and sees Ion Kynne, one of her friends from the Wandering Traders of Arcaena, walking towards her.*

**Ion:** You're a master of Flora Magic, why are you growing your plants the traditional way?
**Mint:** I got this plant from Bauserst. I was told it was fragile to magic. Plus, it's fun to watch it grow slowly.
**Ion:** Fragile to magic? Sounds like a bad plant to me.
**Mint:** Hey! It's a rare species from one of the ancient civilizations. What brings you back here anyway? I thought you guys were going up north towards Aquarin?
**Ion:** Well, that was the plan, until Ted decided he wanted more of your Phthalo Roots and went back while we were in Ignia. It's been like a week since he strayed from the group so we went back to look for him.
**Mint:** Ted? He hasn't come by here since your last visit. Maybe he got lost on the way back?
**Ion:** That's impossible, that bear knows every path in Arcaena. He probably could map the entire continent blindfolded if given the chance.
**Mint:** Hmm... Didn't you say last time that an unknown group ambushed you while traveling? Could it be them? 
**Ion:** ... That's what I've been worrying about too... In that case, would you help us find him?
**Ion:** Let's travel again, just like old times.
**Mint:** I would like to, but the village is going to celebrate the Crops Festival in a few days, so I can't really go wandering around kingdoms.
**Ion:** That's unfortunate... Well, we'll be staying in Rhilekos City while we search for him, so if you were to cross paths with Ted, please bring him there.
**Mint:** Alright! 

*Ion and her group left shortly after their conversation with Mint.*

> [!NOTE] Notes
> - The group that attacked the wanderers is still unknown.
> - [[Phthalo Roots]] is a special crop that only grows in the soil of [[Vilebloom Forest]]; it's sweet despite its earthy appearance.
> - The [[Crops Festival]] is an occasion in [[Lenben Village]]. It is celebrated before winter to celebrate their harvests this year.
