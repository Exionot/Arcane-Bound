**Character/s**:
- [[Mint Evergreen]]
- [[Aura Brillfer]]
- [[Ruby Rossilia]]

**Location**: [[Aura's Apartment]]
**Time of Occurrence**: 2405, 6th of Early Winter

*It has been a few days since Mint was transported back into Synthspire.*

**Mint:** *Mmmmm... There's nothing left to do here...*
**Mint:** Hey Aura.
**Aura:** What's up?
**Mint:** I was wondering if I could go outside and wander around the city?
**Aura:** Hmm... You have been stuck here for a couple of days now, and the gang situation has remained fairly the same since then...
**Aura:** I guess we could take a stroll around the city.
**Mint:** Really?? Yay!

*As Mint and Aura get dressed before they leave, Aura puts a cap on Mint's head.*

**Aura:** Wear this.
**Mint:** A cap?
**Aura:** Though they did not saw your face during the chase, we still don't know whether they would recognize you from your hair.
**Mint:** Ah, i see.
**Mint:** *Her cap... It's completely covered in her scent.*

*The two walk together showing Mint different places in the city. As they wander around, something catches Aura's eyes.*

**Aura:** Hey.
**Mint:** Hm?
**Aura:** Are you willing to dye your hair?
**Mint:** M-my hair?? What for?
**Aura:** So you could roam around more freely.
**Mint:** Hmm... I guess if it does make me move around more freely, then I'm all for it!
**Aura:** Great. The salon I go to just so happens to be nearby.

*Mint and Aura arrived at the salon.*

**Ruby:** Welcome, welc-. Ah, Aura! Welcome back!
**Aura:** Yeah, it's been a while hasn't it?
**Ruby:** Oh? Who's this with you? A friend?
**Mint:** *!! Wait, we haven't planned out how to explain my situation yet!*
**Aura:** O-of sorts yeah... This is Mint, she's actually a daughter of a distant relative of mine. I'm just taking care of her for the meantime while they're away...
**Mint:** *Nice save Aura!*
**Ruby:** I see... Well. what brings you here? A haircut perhaps?
**Aura:** I was actually thinking of getting Mint's hair dyed.
**Ruby:** Really? But it's such a nice shade of green.
**Aura:** She just want something less eye-catching for the meantime.
**Ruby:** I see, how about a pale black?
**Aura:** Does that sound good to you Mint?
**Mint:** Yeah!

*Ruby proceeds to dye Mint's hair, the sun was already setting when Mint and Aura was about to leave.*

**Aura:** Thanks again Ruby. You should come over some time.
**Ruby:** If I get my hand free, I will!

*Mint and Aura left the salon and is now walking home together.*

**Mint:** Both of you seem close.
**Aura:** Oh, Ruby? I used to come there often because I kept on changing my hairstyle when I first moved here.
**Mint:** *Aura with different hairstyles... I wonder what she looked like then.*

*Mint fidgets with her hair as Aura looks at her.*

**Aura:** That color suites you really well.
**Mint:** Really?
**Aura:** Yeah, it makes your eyes stand out more.
**Mint:** *!! She's so kind... Is she like this to everybody?*
