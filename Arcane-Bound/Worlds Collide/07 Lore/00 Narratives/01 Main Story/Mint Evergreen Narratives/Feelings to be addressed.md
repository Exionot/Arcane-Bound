**Character/s**:
- [[Mint Evergreen]]
- [[Renze Luna]]

**Location**: [[Aura's Apartment]]
**Time of Occurrence**: 2405, 26th of Early Winter

*The sun is close to setting, Mint is playing around with magic, out of nowhere, somebody knocks on the front door, Mint's ears shifts to the sound as she stumbles around while concealing her staff. Before she could ask, a familiar voice calls out to her on the other side of the door.*

**Renze:** Mint? Are ya there?
**Mint:** *Renze?*

*Mint opens the door, it's Renze standing in front of her.*

**Renze:** Heyya, Mint!
**Mint:** Hello, come in.
**Mint:** Aura isn't home yet.
**Renze:** It's fine, it's fine, I actually wanted to talk to you.
**Mint:** Me?

*Mint and Renze sit together in the couch.*

**Renze:** Mint... Do you hate me by any chance?
**Mint:** W-what??
**Renze:** Well... I noticed that every time I go here and talk to Aura, you just stare at me menacingly.
**Renze:** I could practically feel the stare from my back...
**Mint:** Ah! I don't hate you, so please don't worry!
**Mint:** *Did I actually do that?? I don't think I stare at her that much...*
**Renze:** Ah what a relief... I really thought ya were trying to pierce me with your laser eyes.
**Mint:** I'm sorry... 
**Mint:** *Heh, if only she knew I could actually do that.*
**Renze:** Ya know, to me, ya looked like a jealous girlfriend trying to get her girl back.

*Mint turns away from Renze and blushes slightly.*

**Mint:** ...
**Renze:** ... Wait actually?
**Mint:** N-no! We're cousins from a distant relative remember?
**Renze:** You're not from here Mint. 
**Mint:** H-huh? W-what do you mean?
**Renze:** You're one of those people that keep popin' up, no?
**Mint:** *How does she know? Was I too obvious?*
**Mint:** How did you know...?
**Renze:** Y'see, Aura and I have been close friends since the start of high school, I'm also close with some of her relatives, so I was surprised when I saw that she has a cousin I didn't knew about.
**Renze:** Also, I noticed that ya weren't really familiar with any of the places here.
**Mint:** I see... I guess it was really obvious.
**Renze:** Now... with your disguise exposed, lemme ask again...
**Renze:** Are the two of ya datin'?
**Mint:** No...
**Renze:** What?! Well, do you like her?
**Mint:** ... I'm not sure...
**Renze:** What'd ya mean you're not sure?
**Mint:** I do enjoy her company, but I'm still figuring out my feelings for her...
**Renze:** Aww... What a letdown...

*Renze takes a glimpse at her phone and notices the time.*

**Renze:** Welp! I guess I got to get goin now.

*Both of them stand up and approach the front door.*

**Mint:** Thank you for the chat. Also, please keep this a secret... even to Aura.
**Renze:** Don't worry, I ain't tellin' a soul. Aura must've had her reason why she took you in. 
**Mint:** *A reason...*
**Renze:** By the way!
**Mint:** ?
**Renze:** I'd hurry up figuring out my feelings if I were you.
**Renze:** 'Cause if your not fast enough, somebody closer might take your spot...
**Renze:** Anyway! I'll go now!

*Renze leaves and shut the door before Mint can react leaving her standing alone in the apartment.*

**Mint:** *Someone... closer?*
