**Character/s**: [[Mint Evergreen]], [[Aura Brillfer]]
**Location**: [[Aura's Apartment]]
**Time of Occurrence**: 2405, 1st of Early Winter
**Related**: [[My house]]

*The sun is about to set. Mint follows Aura into a building complex.*

**Mint:** Um, where are we going?
**Aura:** We're going to where I live.
**Mint:** Oh, I thought you were just going to tell me how to get out of the city.
**Aura:** The city is way too big to get to any exit gates by foot, and it gets really cold during winter nights. I can guide you once the night passes. Your clothes might grab attention too.
**Mint:** *My clothes...?*

*Aura opens the door to her apartment. It is completely different from what Mint is used to; the walls are not made from wood or stone, and lights emit from the ceiling instead of using lamps and candles.*

**Mint:** Whoa... this is nothing like the inns I've been to. 
**Mint:** *Looks like she is the only person here, I can only smell her scent in the entire room.*

*Mint looks around the room; she notices a photo of Aura with her name printed on it.*

**Mint:** *Aura Brillfer, huh... I'll remember that.*

*Mint lies on the couch. She sinks a tiny bit, the mattress' softness surrounds her body. Mint is completely amazed by its softness.*

**Aura:** It's just a couch, have you not sat on one before?
**Mint:** This is the softest couch I've ever been on! Not even the King's couch can beat this one!

*Aura stands by the door watching as Mint marvels at the couch.*
