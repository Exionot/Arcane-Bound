**Aura:** Mint, I'm back.
**Mint:** *Aura?*

**Mint:** Who are you?

**Mint:** Lies! Aura is not a mage.
**Mint:** Identify yourself, imposter.

*Aura pushes the door fully open, revealing herself holding the staff to Mint.*

**Aura:** It **is** me! Can you please let go of the staff now?

**Aura:** Geez, quite hostile today are we? What's going on?
**Mint:** Sorry, I thought I was going to be attacked.
**Aura:** Attacked? What made you think that?
**Mint:** Well... letting a staff through a door first is a sign of hostility in our world...
**Aura:** I see, I'm sorry, I didn't know.

*Aura leans the staff on the wall and places her hand onto Mint's head and pets her.*
