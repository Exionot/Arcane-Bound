**Character/s**:
- [[Mint Evergreen]]
- [[Aura Brillfer]]

**Location**: [[Aura's Apartment]]
**Time of Occurrence**: 2405, 7th of Winter

*It is night, Mint is analyzing the staff Aura has brought home. The staff has a sleek design, its handle is made from some sort of blue gem crystal, the base is flared and pointed, and has a blue Arcaenian Band.*

**Mint:** The owner of this staff, where are they?
**Aura:** I have no idea, station said they found it in one of the reported areas.
**Aura:** Do you recognize this staff?
**Mint:** No, it's just, when a mage is not using their staff to cast, they usually conceal their staff right away.

*Mint takes a closer look at the Arcaenian Band attached to the staff, the Aquarin insignia is stamped just above the Arcaenic Crystal.*

**Mint:** This one is from the Aquarin Kingdom.
**Aura:** You can tell where it's from just by looking at it?
**Mint:** Look here, this is the insignia of Aquarin.
**Aura:** "Aquarin"? What's that?
**Mint:** Aquarin is one of the largest kingdoms in Arcaena, they have some of the most powerful mages in the world.
**Mint:** I'm guessing this staff has a better magical output basing on where it's from. 
**Aura:** Can you use this staff by any chance?
**Mint:** I could, but what should I do?
**Aura:** Blow a gust wind I guess?

*Mint puts her hand on the grip of the staff, points besides Aura and casts a wind spell. The output of the staff was unexpectedly high that it blows both of them back a step.*

**Mint:** Woah, that was harsher than I expected...
**Aura:** Is there anything else that it can do?
**Mint:** Well, since it's base is pointed, I think it's main use is to cast spells that affect an area rather than direct projectiles.

*Mint casts a spell on the staff as she throws it down base first, instead of piercing the floor the staff levitates inches from the ground, Mint then hovers both of her hands near the head of the staff and chants a spell.*

**Mint:** Warmth!

*A breeze of warm air surrounds the room, the room filled with the chilly breeze of winter now replaced with the warm air of the spell.*

**Aura:** Wow, you could do that?
**Mint:** With my staff no, mine has a flat base so I would just end up heating the floor.
**Aura:** That would be problematic huh.
**Aura:** Well, you can just hold onto that staff for the meantime.
**Mint:** Where will I put it though?
**Aura:** Can you not make it disappear like with your staff?
**Mint:** No.
**Aura:** Why?
**Mint:** Staves are usually enchanted with a spell that only allows the one who casts it to conceal it.
**Aura:** Well, just take care of the staff while we try to find its owner.
**Mint:** Alright.
