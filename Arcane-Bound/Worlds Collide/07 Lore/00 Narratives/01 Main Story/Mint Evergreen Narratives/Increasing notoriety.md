**Character/s**: 
- [[Mint Evergreen]]
- [[Aura Brillfer]]

**Location**: 
- [[Amphere Square]]
- [[Aura's Apartment]]

**Time of Occurrence**: 2405, 3rd of Early Winter

*It is almost noon. Mint has found herself sitting on a bench in Amphere Square, not knowing where to go.*

**Mint:** *Sigh* *It's impossible to navigate this big city if every corner just looks the same!*

*A Vulpes Virken and her kit sits next to Mint in the bench. The kit looks at her with curiosity. Mint grows tiny flowers on her palm and gives it to the kit.*

**Kit:** Whoaah... Mom, look! She grew flowers on her hands!
**Mom:** I'm sure she did sweety.
**Mom:** Do you do magic tricks?
**Mint:** Ah, sort of I guess...
**Mint:** *I forgot these people are oblivious to magic... Better not catch any attention.*

*Mint looks around and notices white coated individuals, almost similar to Aura's uniform, observing her.*

**Mint:** *Why? They've been staring at me for a while now...*

*Mint gets up from the bench, she flips up her hood and begins to go back into the alleyway.*

**Mint:** *Are they after me? Is these the people Aura was talking about?*

*The coated individuals continue to follow Mint into the alleyway.*

**Coated Figure:** Stop right there feline!
**Mint:** What do you want?
**Coated Figure:** We need to confirm your identity, you have been tagged for suspicious behavior!
**Mint:** *I guess that's my cue to run.*

*Mint casts a large burst of wind bellow her, launching her upward into one of the rooftops of the buildings. She overhears their radio call from the echoes of the alleyway.*

**Coated Figure:** Calling other responders in the area, we have a possible member of a gang organization heading to one of the rooftops in Amp Square!

**Mint:** *How did they know?*

*Mint continues to run to different alleyways yet the coated individuals keep on catching up to her. And then suddenly, as she was running through one of the alleyways, a mysterious hand grabs her. Mint immediately summons her staff however, to her surprise, it was Aura who grabbed her.*

**Mint:** Aura!? How did you find m-

*Aura blocks Mint's mouth with her hand.*

**Aura:** Shhh...

*The coated figures pass by their location and both of them exit the alleyway quietly. They both take the underground passage back into Aura's Apartment.*

**Aura:** Where did you go? And what did you do?
**Mint:** I- I don't know! Yesterday I woke up in my world and then earlier I was back here again! I tried to find you but I just got lost!
**Aura:** Why did they pursue you?
**Mint:** I have no idea! They were already observing me when I noticed! They tried to confirm my identity so I ran away!

*Aura's radio suddenly sparks to life.*

**Radio:** This is Point Delta, 22nd street is clear, no signs of the suspect.
**Aura:** I need to get back there.
**Aura:** Stay here. Do not go anywhere, you got it?
**Mint:** G-got it!

*Aura slams the door shut and locks it as she leaves, leaving Mint alone in the apartment sitting on Aura's couch.*
