# Missing merchant



**Character/s**: Mint Evergreen, Ion Kynne
**Location**: Lenben Village
**Time of Occurrence**: 2405, Late Autumn

*Mint is tending to her plants until suddenly a familiar voice shouts her name. Mint turns around and sees Ion Kynne, one of her friends from the Wandering Traders of Arcaena. *

**Ion:** You're a master of Flora Magic why are you growing you plants that way?
**Mint:** I got it in Bauserst, I was told it was fragile to magic. Plus, it's fun to watch it grow slowly.
**Ion:** Fragile to magic? Sounds like a bad plant to me.
**Mint:** Hey! It's a rare specie from one of the ancient civilizations. What brings you back here anyway? I thought you guys were going up north to Aquarin?
**Ion:** Well, that was the plan, until Ted decided he wanted more of your Phthalo Roots and went back while we were in Ignia. It's been like a week since he strayed from the group so we went here to look for him.
**Mint:** Ted? He hasn't come by here since your last visit. Maybe he got lost on the way back?
**Ion:** That's impossible, that bear is as ancient as bricks, he practically knows every path in Arcaena.
**Mint:** Could it be he got captured by the people that attacked you last time?
**Ion:** Hmm... I hope not, can you help us find him? Travel with us again, just like old times.
**Mint:** I would like to, but the village is going to celebrate the Crops Festival in a few days, so I can't really go wandering around the kingdom.
**Ion:** That's unfortunate... Well, we'll be staying in Rhilekos City while we look for him, so if you were to cross paths with Ted, please bring him there.
**Mint:** Alright! 

*Ion and her group left after their conversation with Mint*

> [!NOTE] Notes
> - The group that attacked the wanderers is still unknown.
> - Phthalo Roots is a special crop that only grows in the soil of Vilebloom Forest, it's sweet despite its earthy appearance.
> - The Crops Festival is an occasion in Lenben Village. It is celebrated before winter to celebrate their harvests this year.

# The convergence (Mint Evergreen)



**Character/s**: Mint Evergreen
**Location**: Lenben Village, Amphere Square
**Time of Occurrence**: 2405, Early Winter

*It's the first day of winter, tiny snowflakes falls from the sky covering the lush green grass of Lenben Village with a thin layer of white snow.*

*Mint roams around the village watching as the snow falls. Mint turns around. The village has disappeared, in its place is a dark alleyway of a city. Despite travelling the entirety of the Verdthorne Kingdom, she does not recognize this place, the architecture is almost alien to her. Confused, Mint exits the alleyway only to see a bustling city before her.*

**Mint:** *How did I end up here? I was at Lenben just a moment ago. Teleportation magic? No, there's no such thing in Arcaena. Illusion Magic? But I don't sense any mana anywhere, in fact, I don't feel mana anywhere...*

*Mint closes her eyes and focuses, she exerts a small amount of mana hoping to disturb any stagnant mana lingering around her but the only thing she detects is radio silence, almost as if mana did not exists.*

**Mint:** Where am I...

# A fellow Feline



**Character/s**: Mint Evergreen, Aura Brillfer
**Location**:  Amphere Square
**Time of Occurrence**: 2405, Early Winter
**Related**: Defying orders

*Mint finds herself in what it seems like the center of a bustling city, the area is loud, people walk by at every moment, their outfits are more diverse, almost casual yet different to arcaenian outfits.*

**Mint:** I really need to find a way to get out of here...

*Mint remains lost, she looks in between the crowd and sees it. Ears and tail just like hers, a fellow feline is within her sight. Mint squeezes against the crowd catching up to the feline she saw.*

**Mint:** E-excuse me!

*Aura stops walking and turns around, her bodyguards raises their weapon and aims it towards Mint.*

**Bodyguard:** Back off civilian!

*Mint steps back and before she could summon her weapon, Aura commands her bodyguards to lower their weapons.*

*Mint focuses and checks aura for any traces of mana, but to no avail, there's nothing.*

**Aura:** Are you in need of any assistance?

*Mint being too focused, unknowingly misses Aura's question and stares at her awkwardly.*

**Aura:** Are you lost?

*Mint returns back to her senses.*

**Mint:** Ah! Yes, it seems like I got suddenly transported here. 
**Mint:** Where are we right now?
**Aura:** This is Amphere Square of Fluxpoint City.
**Mint:** I didn't know Arcaena had such developed cities like this.

*Aura commands her bodyguards to return to base. The bodyguards walk away, leaving Aura with Mint.*

**Aura:** Follow me.

*Mint, confused, nods and follows Aura.*

**Mint:** *She might have answers to what's happening so I can get home.*

# Confrontation



**Character/s**: Mint Evergreen, Aura Brillfer
**Location**: Aura's Apartment
**Time of Occurrence**: 2405, Early Winter

*Mint is laying on Aura's couch amazed by it's softness.*

*Aura locks the door to her apartment. Mint's ear shifts towards the sound. She tenses up and sits up.*

**Aura:** Tell me, what does your group want with Synthspire?
**Mint:** Group?
**Aura:** There have been multiple cases of missing people in the entire continent the past few days, and coincidentally there also have been reports of people appearing with absolutely no records even in the biggest database in the world. And you, just so happens to fit the description.

*Mint, shocked, the realization finally settles in that she had been transported to another world.* 

**Mint:** I... I don't know... 
**Mint:** I was just enjoying the snow in our village and then suddenly... I was here... I got transported into a completely different world-
**Aura:** Stop playing with me feline! What do your people need with the people of Synthspire!?

*Mint stands in frustration*

**Mint:** I SAID I DON'T KNOW! 
**Mint:** I just want to get back home...

*Aura, unconvinced, she points her wrist at Mint. The band on Aura's wrists expands, revealing what seems to be a weapon.*

**Aura:** Don't make me do this.

*Mint backs off, summoning her staff and pointing it at Aura.*

**Mint:** I really have no idea of what you are talking about miss! I just want to get back home!

*Aura, bewildered, seeing a long staff suddenly appearing from thin air, she fires an electrocuting dart in an attempt of immobilizing Mint.*

*Mint barely dodges the dart, it hits the wall behind her.*

**Mint:** *Thundra magic? How is she doing that, I don't sense any mana from her.*

*Mint casts a sharp gust of wind towards Aura. It pushes Aura back scratching her cheek.*

*Aura touches her cheek, a thin streak of blood traces from the scratch to her fingers.*

**Aura:** Where did you get this tech?
**Mint:** Tech? You mean my spell?
**Aura:** Stop making a fool of me feline! I know you are using projection technology!
**Mint:** I don't even know what those words mean! 
**Mint:** Let me show you.

*Mint approaches Aura and conceals her staff.*

*Aura repels away from Mint's hand.*

**Mint:** Stay still.

*Mint places her hand on Aura's wounded cheek.* 

*Mint casts a healing spell, a bright green light emits from Mint's hand, healing the scratch on Aura's face.*

*Mint grabs the mirror on the counter, showing Aura her face. The scratch has healed.*

**Mint:** Do you believe me now?
**Aura:** H-how is this possible... Who are you?
**Mint:** Just call me Mint, Mint Evergreen.

# The convergence... again



**Character/s**: Mint Evergreen
**Location**: Aura's Apartment, Vilebloom Forest
**Time of Occurrence**: 2405, Early Winter

*It's night, Aura is rummaging through her closet.*

**Mint:** Why do I need to change my outfit?
**Aura:** You'll get arrested if you looked like that in public, and it's winter, you'll freeze up if that's what you're going to wear outside.
**Mint:** I can heat myself up just fine! Why would they arrest me by my outfit? Your world is not very welcoming to it's visitors.
**Aura:** That's what I'm worried about too, they seem to be after your people.
**Mint:** *Ted probably got transported here too, I need to find him, before he gets arrested, but how?*

*Aura hands Mint a set of clothes. It's a black shirt, pants, and shorts with a blue jacket.*

**Aura:** Take these, it'll probably fit on you.
**Mint:** Thanks!
**Aura:** Also, take off your accessories. Even if those aren't actually gold, you'll probably attract some attention with those.
**Mint:** Ah, these?

*Mint hovers her hand over her earrings and casts a spell. Her earrings slowly turned from a shiny gold to a silver-like material.*

**Aura:** Wha- How'd you do that?
**Mint:** Illusion magic! It's not that big so I can change their look constantly without a problem.
**Aura:** Ah, right, magic. I still can't believe you're able to do that.
**Mint:** I mean, even just the thought that I'm in another world is already overwhelming.

*Mint takes off her hair clip and places it on the table.*

**Mint:** Oh, I'll take these off actually.
**Aura:** Why? Couldn't you just change its look?
**Mint:** It's heavy on my hair! It just feels like it's constantly being pulled.

*Mint lets out a big yawn, and stretches.*

**Aura:** We should probably rest now. Where to you want to rest?
**Mint:** I'll sleep where you sleep!
**Aura:** Well, my bed is over in that room, I'll probably just lay here on the couch.

*Mint looks at Aura's eyes.*

**Mint:** That's not what I meant...

*Aura looks at Mint in surprise, her cheeks subtly blushes.*

**Aura:** ...Seriously?
**Mint:** Yes! It's tradition in our world for felines to snuggle together!
**Aura:** Alright... fine...
**Mint:** *She's so easy to get flustered, it's so cute hehe.*

*Mint rests peacefully on Aura's bed beside her. She wakes up on a brand new day, she hears a faint voice waking her up.*

**???:** Hey... wake up..

*Mint opens her eyes, what she sees is not the ceiling of Aura's room, but the sky. Mint quickly sits up and looks around, the white snow covers the trees and grass, but they are not their usual green, but a bright teal color.*

**Mint:** *I recognize this place... Vilebloom forest? I'm back in Arcaena? How? Was all of that a dream?*

**Location**: Vilebloom Forest
**Time of Occurrence**: 2405, Early Winter

*Mint wakes up somewhere in Vilebloom Forest, suddenly, she hears a rustle from within the bushes. Mint summons her staff and scans the mana around her. She feels something faint presence lingering behind her.*

**Mint:** *!! Behind??*

*Mint swiftly turns around and point her staff, ready to attack.*

**Clair:** You have quite the instinct, I didn't expect you to see me.

*The person she sees before her is a devil with cyan hair. Mint realizes who she is up against.*

**Mint:** You... You're the devil of the forest!

*Mint slowly creates distance between her and the devil.*

**Clair:** No need to be scared, I'm not going to hurt you. I'm more curious about your outfit.

**Mint:** *This is what Aura gave me... so it wasn't a dream...*

*Mint raises her head, the devil is nowhere to be seen. In an instant, something pulls on her staff, disarming her. The devil comes out from behind Mint and binds her with silk-like threads.*

**Clair:** Let's talk somewhere more cozy.

# Devil's intent



**Character/s**: Mint Evergreen, Clair Tempest
**Location**: Vilebloom Forest
**Time of Occurrence**: 2405, Early Winter

*Mint finds herself in front of a fireplace of an old cabin. Silk-like threads bind her hands and feet.*

**Mint:** What do you want from me?
**Clair:** Your clothes... they're not from this world.
**Mint:** *!! How does she know? Was she also transported?*
**Clair:** I lost something in that... other world. A book of mine, without it, I cannot roam these lands freely.
**Mint:** Couldn't you just use a map?
**Clair:** That's not my problem. That book keeps **her** away.
**Mint:** *Her? Is there another devil in this forest?*
**Clair:** I need your help, if you were to be transported again, please find it.
**Mint:** If you needed my help then why am I-

*The string that bound Mint disappears before she could even finish talking. Suddenly, the atmosphere of the room changes, something else is with them.*

**Clair:** Go...
**Mint:** What? I still have so many questions!

*The fireplace suddenly extinguishes itself.*

**Clair:** Run north from here and you'll find your village...
**Mint:** Why are you releasing me now? Are you behind the mysterious transportations? Wh-
**Clair:** STOP TRYING TO ARGUE! 

*A powerful gust of wind pushes Mint to the door. Mint watches as Clair's hair slowly turns into an ashen grey. Clair shouts at her, her eyes glow menacingly.*

**Clair:** JUST GO!

*Mint runs away from the cabin into the woods.*

**Mint:** *What was that about? I need to go back to the village.*

# Bear-on collision



**Character/s**: Mint Evergreen, Ted Ursa, Victoria Veloux Canidel
**Location**: Vilebloom Forest, Outside of Lenben Village
**Time of Occurrence**: 2405, Early Winter
**Related**: Feline encounter

*Mint is walking through the woods of Vilebloom Forest. She continues to walk, looking for a path she can follow, and then she saw it.*

**Mint:** *Finally a path! I can finally go home now.*

*Mint enters the dirt path through the bushes. But suddenly, when she looked to her left, a bear is approaching her at full sprint.*

**Mint:** H-hey wait! Slow down!

*In a blink of an eye, mint summons her staff and casts a barrier in front of her. Not being able to stop in time, the bear hits the barrier, the impact was able to push Mint a step back.*

**Mint:** What... are you doing?! Why are you running so fast in these narrow paths-

*Mint takes a closer look at the bear and recognizes him.*

**Mint:** Ted?! What are you doing here??
**Mint:** *Maybe he wasn't transported after all?*
**Ted:** Mint! I'm so sorry! We're actually rushing to your house.
**Mint:** We?

*Mint looks to the side of Ted and sees a Vulpes Virken standing behind him. Just from the way the girl stood, Mint immediately recognizes her from a higher social status.*

**Mint:** Whoa... You're a fancy girl, I can feel it. Which royal family are you fro-
**Mint:** Ted... Ion came to me looking for you a couple days ago. You didn't kidnap a member of the royalties and disguised it as "going back for phthalo roots" did you?
**Ted:** No, of course not! This is Vi, she's not of any royalties. Well... at least not in this world.
**Mint:** *Not in this world? She's not from here?*
**Mint:** Did both of you got transported suddenly?
**Ted:** Wait, how did you know we got transported?
**Mint:** I got transported to a different world as well.
**Ted:** You got transported too?? How come i did not saw you in the detention area?
**Mint:** *He got locked up? I guess Aura wasn't lying after all.*
**Mint:** A nice lady took me in. 
**Mint:** *Well after she tried to fight me that is...*
**Mint:** She said people were after us.
**Ted:** Wait, but why are they after us? We haven't done anything.
**Mint:** *That's what I'm wondering too...*
**Mint:** You should probably head straight to Rhilekos City for now, Ion said they would stay there until they find you.
**Ted:** Are you not going with us?
**Mint:** I need to check on the village first, just to make sure nobody got transported too.
**Mint:** I'll try to catch up with you after I check the village.

*Mint and Ted wave they goodbyes as they split up their paths.*
