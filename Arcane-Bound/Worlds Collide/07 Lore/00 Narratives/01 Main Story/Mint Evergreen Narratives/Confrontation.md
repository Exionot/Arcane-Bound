**Character/s**: [[Mint Evergreen]], [[Aura Brillfer]]
**Location**: [[Aura's Apartment]]
**Time of Occurrence**: 2405, Early Winter

*Mint is laying on Aura's couch amazed by it's softness.*

*Aura locks the door to her apartment. Mint's ear shifts towards the sound. She tenses up and sits up.*

**Aura:** Tell me, what does your group want with Synthspire?
**Mint:** Group?
**Aura:** There have been multiple cases of missing people in the entire continent the past few days, and coincidentally there also have been reports of people appearing with absolutely no records even in the biggest database in the world. And you, just so happens to fit the description.

*Mint, shocked, the realization finally settles in that she had been transported to another world.* 

**Mint:** I... I don't know... 
**Mint:** I was just enjoying the snow in our village and then suddenly... I was here... I got transported into a completely different world-
**Aura:** Stop playing with me feline! What do your people need with the people of Synthspire!?

*Mint stands in frustration*

**Mint:** I SAID I DON'T KNOW! 
**Mint:** I just want to get back home...

*Aura, unconvinced, she points her wrist at Mint. The band on Aura's wrists expands, revealing what seems to be a weapon.*

**Aura:** Don't make me do this.

*Mint backs off, summoning her staff and pointing it at Aura.*

**Mint:** I really have no idea of what you are talking about miss! I just want to get back home!

*Aura, bewildered, seeing a long staff suddenly appearing from thin air, she fires an electrocuting dart in an attempt of immobilizing Mint.*

*Mint barely dodges the dart, it hits the wall behind her.*

**Mint:** *Thundra magic? How is she doing that, I don't sense any mana from her.*

*Mint casts a sharp gust of wind towards Aura. It pushes Aura back scratching her cheek.*

*Aura touches her cheek, a thin streak of blood traces from the scratch to her fingers.*

**Aura:** Where did you get this tech?
**Mint:** Tech? You mean my spell?
**Aura:** Stop making a fool of me feline! I know you are using projection technology!
**Mint:** I don't even know what those words mean! 
**Mint:** Let me show you.

*Mint approaches Aura and conceals her staff.*

*Aura repels away from Mint's hand.*

**Mint:** Stay still.

*Mint places her hand on Aura's wounded cheek.* 

*Mint casts a healing spell, a bright green light emits from Mint's hand, healing the scratch on Aura's face.*

*Mint grabs the mirror on the counter, showing Aura her face. The scratch has healed.*

**Mint:** Do you believe me now?
**Aura:** H-how is this possible... Who are you?
**Mint:** Just call me Mint, Mint Evergreen.
