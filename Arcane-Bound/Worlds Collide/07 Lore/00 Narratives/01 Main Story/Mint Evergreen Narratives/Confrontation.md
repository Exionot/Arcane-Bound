**Character/s**: 
- [[Mint Evergreen]]
- [[Aura Brillfer]]

**Location**: [[Aura's Apartment]]
**Time of Occurrence**: 2405, 1st of Early Winter

*Mint continues to marvel at the couch's softness.*

*A click sounds from the door where Aura is standing. Mint's ear shifts towards the sound. She tenses and sits up; her body shouting that something is wrong.*

**Aura:** Tell me, where are you from?
**Mint:** I'm from Lenben Village of Verdthorne.
**Aura:** What? Where is that?
**Mint:** It's in the Central Lands.
**Aura:** Central... Lands?

*Aura looks at Mint with a confused expression.*

**Mint:** ...Yeah? It's in the middle of Arcaena, in the kingdom of Flora and Vit-
**Aura:** Hold it. This is making less and less sense. Arcaena? Verdthorne? What are you talking about?
**Aura:** What country are you from? Are you a Neonian? Republican? A Chloran?
**Mint:** What's a 'country'...?
**Mint:** *Am I not in Arcaena? Was I summoned into a different world?*

*Aura glares at Mint; her confused face now mixed with annoyance.*

**Aura:** Enough! Drop the act. What does your group want?
**Mint:** Group...?
**Aura:** There have been multiple cases of missing people the past week, and coincidentally there also have been reports of people appearing with absolutely no records even in the biggest databases in the world. It has been stated that this is the works of an unknown criminal organization.
**Aura:** And you keep on spouting words that do not make sense. If you're not going to help, you can continue your explanation on the station.

*Aura points her wrist at Mint. A band on her wrist expands, revealing what seems to be a weapon.*

**Mint:** Wait! I am not a criminal! I was just enjoying the cold wind in our village, and then-
**Aura:** I've heard enough!

*A dart releases out of the band on Aura's wrist. Mint barely dodges; it hits the wall behind her. The dart lets out a faint crackling sound, similar to the zapping noises of Thundra Magic.*

**Mint:** *Thundra Magic?! How is that possible? I still don't sense mana anywhere.*
**Mint:** *Well, two can play that game.*

*Mint stands up; summoning her staff and points it at Aura. She summons sparks of electricity out of the tip of her staff, crackling loudly.*

*Aura releases another dart, but Mint blocks it by casting Protection. Mint swiftly casts a gust of wind towards Aura; it pushes her back a few steps. Mint then binds Aura with wind magic and casts a magnetizing spell, Aura's wrist weapon flings out of her hand; disarming her.*

**Aura:** You... How are you doing this? Where did you get this tech?
**Mint:** My... spell?
**Aura:** Stop making a fool of me, feline! I know you are using some sort of illegal technology!
**Mint:** I don't even know what those words mean! 

*Mint looks directly at Aura and notices a scratch on her cheek.*

**Mint:** *Her cheek got scratched... I got carried away by the heat of the moment.*

*Mint conceals her staff; it disappears into thin air.*

**Mint:** Down.
**Aura:** What?

*Mint gestures a downward motion with her hand; the wind forcibly puts Aura in a kneeling position. Mint slowly walks towards Aura, and looks down at her; she places her palm on Aura's cheek, she pulls away from Mint's hand.*

**Mint:** You have a scratch on your cheek, look.

*Mint shows her palm to Aura, a streak of blood traces the scratch on her cheek.*

**Mint:** One should never shed a single drop of blood in the place they call home. 
**Aura:** What's this? Are you mocking me? Let me go and I'll treat it myself.
**Mint:** You're unarmed, there's no point in resisting. Just stay still.

*Mint casts a healing spell. A bright green light emits from Mint's hand; the scratch on Aura slowly fades away as it heals. Mint grabs the mirror on the counter, showing Aura her face. The scratch has disappeared as if it was never there.*

**Mint:** Do you believe me now?
**Aura:** H-how is this possible... Who are you?
**Mint:** Just call me Mint, Mint Evergreen.
