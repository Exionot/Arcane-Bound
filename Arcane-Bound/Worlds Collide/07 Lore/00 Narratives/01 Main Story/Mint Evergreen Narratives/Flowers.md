**Character/s**:
- [[Mint Evergreen]]
- [[Aura Brillfer]]

**Location**: [[Aura's Apartment]]
**Time of Occurrence**: 2405, 3rd of Early Winter

*It is the afternoon. Mint is left alone in Aura's Apartment with nothing to do.*

**Mint:** *Hmm... there's nothing to do here....*
**Mint:** *I guess I'll just look around her place.*

*Mint wanders around Aura's Apartment. As she looks around Aura's bookshelves, she notices a small pot of plants on one of the shelves.*

**Mint:** *A plant?*

*Mint takes out the pot to get a closer look at the plant.*

**Mint:** *It's wilted... Poor thing, she probably forgets to water these.*

*Mint places her hand over the wilted plant and casts a spell. The plant slowly sprung back to life, causing it to bloom.*

**Mint:** *Oh, these are forget-me-nots.*

*Mint places the pot of forget-me-nots on the middle of Aura's coffee table and lays down on Aura's couch.*

**Mint:** *I guess I'll just rest while she's not home yet...*

*Mint closes her eyes and begins to drift into slumber.*

*Hours pass and Mint is awaken by the sound of the door opening.*

**Aura:** Sorry, I didn't mean to wake you up.
**Mint:** It's fine, I was just resting until you get back.
**Mint:** What happened when you went out again earlier?
**Aura:** They continued to look for you for another hour after I dragged you here. Worse of that is, the station plans to increase security, so It's probably better for you to stay indoors for a while.
**Mint:** I'm sorry...
**Aura:** Forget-me-nots? I forgot i had these...
**Mint:** Oh, I saw it in one of your shelves, it was wilted so I tried to give it some life.
**Aura:** These are my favorite flowers. Shame that I wasn't able to take care of it properly. Thanks for bringing it back to life.
**Mint:** *What is this warm feeling...*
**Mint:** N-no worries! I-I just happen to know about gardening.


> [!NOTE] Notes
> I swear forget-me-nots where the first thing that went in my head when I was thinking what plant was in that pot. I did NOT know that they symbolize what they symbolize.
