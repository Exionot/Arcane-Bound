**Character/s**:
- [[Mint Evergreen]]
- [[Aura Brillfer]]

**Location**:
**Time of Occurrence**: 2405, Early Winter

*Mint is strolling through Amphere Square alone as she was about to head back into Aura's apartment, she notices someone familiar.*

**Mint:** *Ah! It's her!*
**Mint:** Auraaa!

*Mint excitedly approaches Aura*

**Aura:** Oh? Hello Mint, what brings you out here?
**Mint:** I was actually on my way back to the apartment but I saw you out here so-

*Aura suddenly receives a message from her radio.*

**Radio:** Calling all points near Amp Square. We have a pursuit of a robber. He was last seen entering 33rd street.
**Aura:** Point Alpha copy, we'll try to block off exits adjacent to 33rd street.
**Aura:** Let's split up.

*Aura proceeds to instruct her bodyguards.*

**Mint:** *She's so serious when she's working...*
**Aura:** Looks like our conversation got cut short, you should head back now.
**Mint:** Okay!

*Mint heads back into Aura's Apartment. Hours pass and Aura returns home, she opens the door very slowly.*

**Mint:** Oh, you're back... Why are you opening it so slowly?
**Aura:** I thought you might be sleeping, I didn't want to disturb you.

*Aura suddenly places her hand onto Mint's head and slowly sifts through her hair.*

**Mint:** *!? What is she doing?!*
**Aura:** Looks like your hair is already growing its color back.
**Mint:** Ah, y-yeah, I guess so...
**Mint:** *Weird, she was so serious and cool earlier, but now she's talking to me so softly... Ahh! Why is my heart beating like crazy??*
