Vi and I were taking a stroll through the capital when I decided to learn more about her. I asked if she had liked anyone before. She denied everything, only claiming that her friends had found her charismatic. She was small. Quiet. So, I teased her about it. A joke, before moving on to the next topic. 

But that was a mistake.

Before I could even finish laughing, my back was already against the wall of some alley.

She leaned in.

Too close.

I could feel her breath. It was warm. Then, she gently brushed her hand against my cheek.

"I'll show you," she said.

My body froze. 

Everything about the moment was too much. The coolness of the wall. Her warm breath. Her hand grazing my cheek. She stared at me with the same flat face, yet it felt different. My chest tightened, like the air around me thinned out suddenly.

"...Feeling flustered yet?" she said softly.

My heart pounded so hard I couldn't catch what she said before that, but it brought me back to my senses.

I shoved her away; both hands on her shoulders. I glared at her, while I tried to reclaim the air I felt I lost.

"N-nope!" I said, louder than I anticipated. "I-it's not working on me."

"Are you sure?" she said, slightly amused. "Your face is red."

I didn't even notice it, but my face flared up without my knowledge. I turned away, unable to do anything else.

"S-shut up!" I protested. "You just caught me off guard!"

That must've been it. My guard was just down. 

After all, there's no way I would fall for someone like her. Right?
