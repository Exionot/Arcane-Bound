# **Basic Information**
**Title**: Tail care
**Character/s**:
- [[Victoria Veloux Canidel]]
- [[Rem Orion]]

**Location**: [[Starveil Retrieval Co.]]
**Time of Occurrence**: Unknown

---

*It is late in the afternoon, Vi comes into Starveil Retrieval just after finishing her morning job.*

**Rem:** Heya boss, you're quite early today.
**Vi:** Shifted out early because we got a contract today don't we?
**Rem:** Yeah, I've already placed the files you asked for at your d- 

*As Vi passes by Rem, she notices something off about Vi's tail.*

**Rem:** Hey wait!!

*Vi turns around confused to what is wrong.*

**Vi:** What is it?
**Rem:** Your tail...

*Vi looks around her tail, nothing seems to be wrong.*

**Vi:** What about it?
**Rem:** It's all tangled and messed up...
**Vi:** Oh yeah, busy day, I take care of it later.
**Rem:** Nope! Come here I'll brush it.

*Rem grabs a chair and begins to brush Vi's tail.*

**Rem:** Seriously, you should take care of your tail more often!
**Vi:** This is a weird obsession of yours, you know that right?
**Rem:** You say that but... you like it anyway don't you?
**Vi:** Alright you caught me.
