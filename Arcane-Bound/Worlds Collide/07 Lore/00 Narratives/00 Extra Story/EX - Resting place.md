# **Basic Information**
**Title**: Resting place
**Character/s**:
- [[Mint Evergreen]]
- [[Aura Brillfer]]

**Location**: [[Aura's Apartment]]
**Time of Occurrence**: Unknown

---

*It is late in the night, Aura is laying on the couch scrolling through her phone, until suddenly Mint sits right besides her, unusually silent. Aura nudges Mint with her legs.*

**Aura:** Mint? You alright?
**Aura:** You're being unusually silent...

*Mint suddenly goes on top of Aura, looking down at her. Mint stares at her with a serious yet slightly flustered face.*

*Aura's face goes red, her eyes jolts everywhere not being able to maintain eye contact.*

**Aura:** W-what's wrong?

*Mint's serious face turns into a smile and crashes and rests on top of Aura hugging her.*

**Mint:** I just wanted to rest here.

*Aura hugs her back and pats the back of Mint's head.*

**Aura:** Alright, you can rest here.
