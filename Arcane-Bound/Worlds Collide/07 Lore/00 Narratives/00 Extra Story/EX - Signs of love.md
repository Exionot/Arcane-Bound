*It is morning. Ion and Vi are having a chat. Then, mid-conversation, Ion stops and stares at Vi.*

**Vi:** ...Is there something on my face?
**Ion:** Shorty.
**Vi:** ? What was that for?
**Ion:** Nothing. Well, we better get ready for the day.

*Ion calls in the other members.*

**Ion:** Alright! We will split up today.
**Ion:** Me and Stelle will stay here, while Ted and Vi go set up shop in the City Market.
**Vi:** We don't get to decide who goes where?
**Ion:** Nope! I've already decided, so you better start walking to the City Market.

*Ion and the group split up. Hours pass. Thoughts begin invading Ion's mind.*

**Ion:** *I wonder if Vi is doing alright there...*

*A customer arrives. Ion almost mistakenly gives the wrong amount of change before Stelle catches her mistake.*

**Ion:** *What if she can't handle that many customers at once...*
**Ion:** *Maybe I should check up on her?*

*Ion starts tapping her foot on the ground. She stands up from her chair, walks in circles, and sits back down, before suddenly shooting up from her seat.*

**Stelle:** Ion, are you alright?
**Ion:** I'll go check on Vi.
**Stelle:** Why? Ted can probably handle most of the job if it gets too crowded there.
**Ion:** I'm just making sure.

*Ion arrives at the City Market. The place is not too crowded but still filled with a lot of people.*

**Ion:** Swap places with me, Ted.
**Ted:** I thought you didn't want this spot?
**Ion:** I changed my mind.
**Ted:** Alright...?

*Ted heads back, leaving Ion and Vi together.*

**Ion:** Watch and learn, little fox. I'll show you how real trading is done.
**Vi:** Can you start by calling me by my name?
**Ion:** Nope!
**Ion:** *Hm... Somehow, it feels more peaceful here despite being crowded.*
**Vi:** Did... something good happen?
**Ion:** Why?
**Vi:** Your tail is wagging quite a lot.
**Ion:** !! A-ah, no. I just do that when I'm bored.
