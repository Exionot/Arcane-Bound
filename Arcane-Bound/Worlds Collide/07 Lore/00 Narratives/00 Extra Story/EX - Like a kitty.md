# **Basic Information**
**Title**: Like a kitty
**Character/s**: 
- [[Mint Evergreen]]
- [[Aura Brillfer]]

**Location**: [[Aura's Apartment]]
**Time of Occurrence**: Unknown

---

*It is the weekend. Mint tends to her potted plants while Aura is relaxing on the sofa, reading a book.*

**Mint:** Ahh... It's so boring being here all day!
**Aura:** Grab a book. you might find something you like.

*Mint sits beside Aura, leaning her head on Aura's shoulder.*

**Mint:** But I don't want to read right now...
**Aura:** Well, what do you wanna do? Ah- hey!

*Mint lays her head onto Aura's lap, trying to grab her attention.*

**Mint:** I want to hang out with you!
**Aura:** With me?
**Aura:** Alright, we'll go outside after I finish this chapter.
**Mint:** But...

*Aura moves away her book and sees Mint's pouting face. She smiles as she closes her book and sets it aside petting Mint on the head.*

**Aura:** Alright, alright, we'll go now.
**Mint:** Yay!
