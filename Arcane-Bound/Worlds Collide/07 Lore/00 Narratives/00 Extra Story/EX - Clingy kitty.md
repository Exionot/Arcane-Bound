# **Basic Information**
**Title**: Clingy Kitty
**Character/s**:
- [[Aura Brillfer]]
- [[Mint Evergreen]]

**Location**: [[Aura's Apartment]]
**Time of Occurrence**: Unknown

---

*It is almost midnight. Aura sits on the floor by the coffee table, finishing work on her laptop. Suddenly, Mint lets out a big yawn from behind her. Aura looks at Mint and sees her eyes almost half-closed.*

**Aura:** Feeling sleepy?
**Mint:** A little bit... *yawn*
**Aura:** You should go to bed. I might take a little longer to finish.
**Mint:** Alright~

*Mint stands up from the couch; she begins walking towards the bedroom.*

**Aura:** Goodnight, Mint.
**Mint:** Goodnight~

*A few minutes pass. Aura is still working on her laptop. To her surprise, Mint steps out of the bedroom.*

**Aura:** Mint? I thought you went to sleep?
**Mint:** Mmmm... 

*Mint sits beside Aura. She leans her head onto Aura's shoulder.*

**Aura:** What's wrong?
**Mint:** I tried to sleep, but I just feel lonely without you...
**Aura:** Alright, alright, I'll finish up soon, okay? Just lie in my lap for now.

*Mint places her head on Aura's lap. She faces away from the screen, and hugs Aura around the stomach. Aura pats Mint's head as she continues to work.*

**Aura:** *She's way too adorable...*
