# **Basic Information**
**Title**: Needy Kitty 2
**Character/s**:
- [[Mint Evergreen]]
- [[Aura Brillfer]]

**Location**: [[Aura's Apartment]]
**Time of Occurrence**: Unknown
**Prose:** [[Need]]

---

*It is a weekend morning. Mint wakes up from her slumber in the living room. She looks around, but Aura, who usually wakes up before her, is nowhere to be found. Mint peeks her head into the bedroom; Aura is still asleep.*

**Mint:** *She's still asleep? That's a first...*

*Mint goes to the bedside, next to Aura. Before saying a word, Mint gazes at a sleeping Aura.*

**Mint:** *I never really get to see her like this. Her eyelashes are long. Her hair is long, and silky...*
**Mint:** *I want to enjoy this a bit more... But I'm also hungry, so time to wake up!*
**Mint:** Aura~ It's morning.
**Aura:** *Groggy* Mmm...

*Mint places her hands on Aura's shoulder, and gently shakes her.*

**Mint:** Aura, wake up- huh?

*Mint places the back of her hand on Aura's neck; her skin is warmer than it should be.*

**Mint:** Aura, you're burning up!
**Aura:** H-huh? Oh. I'm fine, don't worry.

*Aura tries to stand up from the bed, but her legs almost immediately betray her.*

**Mint:** You can't even stand up right now! Just stay there; I'm getting you a towel.

*Mint turns around, but before she could take a step, Aura grabs her by the wrist.*

**Aura:** ...
**Mint:** Aura? What's wrong?
**Aura:** Where are you going?
**Mint:** I'm going to get you a towel to w-
**Aura:** ...

*Aura stares at the floor with a sad expression, holding onto Mint's wrist tighter. Mint comes closer to Aura and gently pulls Aura's head towards her chest. Mint pets the back of Aura's head while hugging her.*

**Mint:** I'm not going anywhere.
**Mint:** *I guess she can be as needy as I am.*
