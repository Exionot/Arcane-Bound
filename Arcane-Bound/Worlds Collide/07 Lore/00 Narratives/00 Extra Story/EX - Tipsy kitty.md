# **Basic Information**
**Title**: Tipsy kitty
**Character/s**:
- [[Aura Brillfer]]
- [[Mint Evergreen]]

**Location**: [[Aura's Apartment]]
**Time of Occurrence**: Unknown

---

*It is a regular weekend evening, Aura share a couch together with Mint resting on her lap. While Aura is busy on her phone, Mint gets up and heads towards the fridge.*

**Mint:** I'm thirsty, what drinks do we have?
**Aura:** I think we have some sodas in there.

*Mint cracks open a cold can and takes a drink.*

**Mint:** Ooh, this one tastes funny but I like it!

*Mint proceeds to drink most of the can before going back to Aura. She then sits on top of Aura's thighs facing her.*

**Mint:** Hehe~
**Aura:** Wha- Hey, what's up? What drink did you get?
**Mint:** Hehe, this one!

*Mint happily shows Aura the drink she got.*

**Aura:** Why are you so happy all of a s-

*As Aura inspect the can, she notices one of the labels say "Hard Soda". Aura immediately takes away the can from Mint setting it aside.*

**Aura:** *She got a hard soda?? I forgot I even had these.*
**Aura:** Hey, are you alright?

*Aura takes a closer look at Mint's pupils.*

**Aura:** *Pupils aren't dilated, so she's not flat out drunk yet.*

*Mint suddenly leans forward closer to Aura's face. She stares directly onto Aura's face. Aura slowly becomes more and more red the longer Mint stares. And then suddenly, Mint gives Aura a kiss on the cheek.*

**Aura:** W-what was that for???

*Mint continues to stare at Aura, now with a satisfied look.*

**Mint:** Hehe~ You're so cute Aura!
**Aura:** I- I do?
