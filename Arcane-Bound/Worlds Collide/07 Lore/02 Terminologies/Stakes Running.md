# **Overview**
**Stakes Running** is a sport sport found in both [[Arcaena]] and [[Synthspire]]. Similar to [[Horse Racing]], races involves two or more [[Virken]]s running over a set distance for competition. However, **Stakes Running** is fully exclusive to [[Virken|Equus Virkens]].

**Stakes Running** has a wide variety in format and different countries and kingdoms have their own specialty of the sport. Variations can include restriction magical or technological intervention, jumping over obstacles, running different distances, or running over different track surfaces.

Though raced purely for entertainment and sport, a major part of **Stake Running** is the gambling associated with it.

# **Characteristics**
Different stake races vary with different characteristics:
## Types
### Standard
The most famous type of **Stakes Running**, [[Virken]]s run through a straight or oval track without any obstacles and without the help of magic or technology.
### Steeplechase
A type of race where Virkens are required to jump over obstacles at set distances. It is conducted only on a handful of locations.  
### Endurance
The longest type of **Stakes Running**, competitors go through controlled long-distance races, the shortest being 10 miles and can go up to over 100 miles. These types of races can last multiple days. Due to the length of these races natural tracks are used.
### Enhanced
Enhanced races is a variation of both standard and steeplechase races, runners go through courses with or without obstacles and is allowed to use magic or technology. This type of races only last under a minute to the discovery and improvement of magic and technology.

## Track
### Turf
Turf tracks are when competitors run through grass grown tracks. This type of track is much more easier for longer distance races.
### Dirt
Dirt tracks don't have any grass composing of a mixture of soil and sand and is primarily used in short-distanced races. The easiness of running this track can vary heavily based on weather and soil mixture.
### Synthetic
Synthetic tracks are only found in [[Synthspire]], these tracks are artificially made to provide consistent performance on all weather conditions. These tracks are the most easiest to run in, often used in long distance course.
### Jump
Jump tracks are exclusively used in steeplechase races, these tracks have obstacles
