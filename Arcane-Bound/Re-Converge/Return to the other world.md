# **Basic Information**
**Title**: Return to the other world
**Character/s**: 
- [[Mint Evergreen]]
- [[Ion Kynne]]

**Location**: Somewhere in [[Aquarin Kingdom]]
**Time of Occurrence**: Unknown

---

*LOCATION UNKNOWN, Mint approaches the Wanderers.*

**Mint:** Ion.
**Ion:** Ah, Mint! Rare to see you here.
**Mint:** I need to speak to you privately.

*Mint and Ion are alone within the forests of Aquarin Kingdom.*

**Ion:** Is this one of your weird magic experiments again?
**Mint:** I guess you could say that...
**Mint:** Well, I figured that you'd want to see **her** again, no?
**Ion:** Her?
**Mint:** Your girlfriend, of course.
**Ion:** ...
**Ion:** Is this what you wanted to talk about? Listen, if this is just a cheap spell that creates a duplicate of her from my memories, then I don't want it okay? I'd rather cherish our memories rather than having a brainless duplicate from a spell-
**Mint:** No, I'm talking about cross dimension travel.
**Ion:** What...?
**Mint:** I did it, I managed to transport something into another world.
**Mint:** Now, I just need to try it on a person.
**Ion:** Uh huh... Wait...
**Ion:** You're using me a as a test monkey??
**Mint:** No no, of course not. I'm using **both** of us as test monkeys.
**Mint:** What do you think? Are you in or are you out?
**Ion:** ... Fine... 

*Mint brings out her staff and a grimoire and prepares to cast the spell.*

**Ion:** If we both die here, I'm going to torment you in the afterlife!
**Mint:** Still no trust in my magic? Fear not! For Ace Mage Mint Evergreen has been training non-stop for almost 15 years!

*With a blink of an eye, the trees overgrown forest before them disappears and is replaced by the cramped walls of an underground tunnel.*

**Mint:** Did it work? Are your limbs still attached?
**Ion:** That could happen???
**Mint:** Well, it's the first time I've transported a living person, so I don't know what to look out for...
**Mint:** Come on, let's get out of this underground passage.

*Mint grabs Ion's hand and drags her to the exit of the tunnel. As they exit the tunnel, they are greeted by the tall buildings of the bustling city of Synthspire.*

**Mint:** **This** is Synthspire!
**Mint:** *I want to see you right away.*
