# Prose Test 2: Something else in mind (Mixed Style)

**Character/s**: [[Ion Kynne]], [[Victoria Veloux Canidel]]
**Location**: Somewhere in [[Sol Capital]]
**Time of Occurrence**: 2405, Winter, Late Night

*It is the peak of midnight. The only sound in the vast silence of Sol Capital is the soft crackle of their small campfire, casting long, dancing shadows against the nearby ruins. Ion and Vi sit close together, the orange glow of the embers reflecting in their eyes as they share a moment of quiet after a long day of travel.*

**Ion:** Good job today.
**Vi:** Same to you.
**Ion:** We really got scammed by that haggler! If you didn't step up I don't know what I w—

*Vi suddenly holds up a hand, her expression sharpening into a cool, professional focus. She leans in toward Ion, her face cutting through the space between them until Ion can feel the faint warmth of her breath.*

**Vi:** Don't move.
**Ion:** H-huh?
**Vi:** Just stay still.

*Vi slides even closer, her serious face mere inches away. Ion’s breath catches in her throat; she can see the flecks of deep purple in Vi’s eyes, more intense than the midnight sky above them. A sudden, thundering pulse echoes in Ion's ears, drownng out the crackle of the fire.*

**Ion:** *W-what is she doing?!*
**Ion:** *!! Don't tell me she's about to...*

*Ion squeezes her eyes shut, her heart hammering against her ribs like a trapped bird. She waits for the touch, for the "kiss kiss" she’s been secretly expecting—until she feels a sharp, quick "snatch" near her ear. Vi pulls back, opening her hand to reveal a small, twitching insect.*

**Vi:** There was a critter in your hair.
**Ion:** O-oh... There was?

*Ion feels her face erupt into a fierce, "beet-red" heat that makes the campfire feel cold by comparison. She frantically avoids Vi’s gaze, her hands fidgeting with the hem of her travel cloak.*

**Vi:** You alright? Your face is red.
**Ion:** Y-yeah... I was just startled...

*Vi lets out a soft, dry chuckle, her eyes sparkling with a mischievous, "noble" wit. She leans back into the shadows, a smug smile playing on her lips.*

**Vi:** Don't tell me... you were expecting something else?
**Ion:** !! N-no! Definitely not!
**Ion:** Gahh! I'm going to sleep now!
**Ion:** A-and you shouldn't stay up so late too!

*Ion quickly scrambles to her feet and rushes toward her tent, her footsteps loud and clumsy in the quiet night. Behind her, the fire continues to crackle, a solitary witness to her total loss of composure.*

**Ion:** *I just don't know what to do every time she's that close to me...*
**Ion:** *Aahhh! My heart won't calm down!*

---

> [!NOTE] Notes
> This "Mixed Style" keeps the dialogue-first format while using the stage directions to provide the "internal monologue" and atmospheric "weight" of a light novel. It turns the simple actions into "Cinematic Beats" that define the emotional stakes of the scene.
